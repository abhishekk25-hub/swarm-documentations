'''Tell Umm el-'Amr''', also known as '''Tell Umm Amer''', is an archaeological site in the [[Nuseirat refugee camp]], [[Gaza Strip]], [[Palestine]]. The site contains the [[Saint Hilarion Monastery]], which was established in the 4th century. Tell Umm el-'Amr (including the monastery) was designated a UNESCO [[World Heritage Site]] in 2024 and was simultaneously added to the [[List of World Heritage in Danger|'In Danger' list]].

==Location==
Tell Umm el-ʿAmr is located in the dune landscapes south of the mouth of the [[Besor Stream|Wadi Ghazzeh]] in the area of the Al Nusairat refugee camp, about {{convert|5.3|mi}} south of Gaza City.<ref>{{cite book |last=Almbaid |first=Rania Filfil |editor-first1=Rami K. |editor-last1=Isaac |editor-first2=C. Michael |editor-last2=Hall |editor-first3=Freya |editor-last3=Higgins-Desbiolles |chapter=Gaza: the missing tourism assets  |title=The Politics and Power of Tourism in Palestine |date=2018 |publisher=Routledge |isbn=9781138592285 |doi=10.4324/9781315740508-12 |pages=137–148}}</ref> The distance to the Mediterranean coast is {{convert|1000|–|2000|ft}}. In the east and southeast of the Tell Umm el-'Amr site, there are palm groves that form a border with the neighboring town of [[Deir al-Balah]]. The archaeological site covers an area of approximately {{convert|46000|sqft}}.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Elter |first1=René |last2=Hassoune |first2=Ayman |title=Le complexe du bain du monastère de Saint Hilarion à Umm el-'Amr, première synthèse architecturale |trans-title=The bath complex of the monastery of Saint Hilarion in Umm el-'Amr, the first architectural synthesis |url=https://journals.openedition.org/syria/474 |journal=Syria |pages=129–144 |language=fr |doi=10.4000/syria.474 |date=2008|issue=85 |url-access=subscription }}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Lampe à huile en forme de pantoufle - Trésors sauvés de Gaza.jpg|thumb|A 13th- or 14th-century lamp in the shape of a slipper discovered at Tell Umm el-Amr and exhibited at ''[[Saved Treasures of Gaza: 5000 Years of History]]'']]
Uncovered by local archaeologists in 1999, the Tell Umm el-'Amr site was active from the 4th to the 8th century and contains Christian artifacts.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tell Umm El-'Amr (Saint Hilarion Monastery) |url=https://www.wmf.org/project/tell-umm-el-amr-saint-hilarion-monastery |access-date=2023-11-29 |website=World Monuments Fund |language=en |archive-date=2023-12-01 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231201071541/https://www.wmf.org/project/tell-umm-el-amr-saint-hilarion-monastery |url-status=live }}</ref> The ancient village of '''Tabatha''' was once located there.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Becker |first=Jeffrey |date=2024-07-27 |title=Tell Umm Amer: a Pleiades place resource |url=https://pleiades.stoa.org/places/458345971 |access-date=2025-07-27 |website=Pleiades: a gazetteer of past places |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal |last=Taha |first=Hamdan |date=2024 |title=Destruction of Cultural Heritage in Gaza |url=https://www.palestine-studies.org/sites/default/files/jqpdf/Jerusalem%20Quarterly%20%28Issue%2097%29%20for%20WEB-1.pdf |journal=Jerusalem Quarterly |issue=97 |doi=10.70190/jq.I97.p45}}</ref> The site consists of [[Saint Hilarion Monastery|the monastery of Saint Hilarion]]; as well as religious buildings (e.g. church, cloister) and all the outbuildings necessary for the life of the monks (e.g. miscellaneous room, dormitory). Additionally, the archaeologists found a hotel complex and baths probably used by visiting pilgrims at the site.<ref name="UNESCO2024" />

===Preservation and protection=== 
In 2013, fundraising was launched in order to be able to carry out excavations on the site and put in place protection measures.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2013-06-04 |title=Sauver Saint-Hilarion à Gaza, le plus vieux monastère de Terre sainte |url=https://www.lepoint.fr/culture/sauver-saint-hilarion-a-gaza-le-plus-vieux-monastere-de-terre-sainte-04-06-2013-1676043_3.php |access-date=2023-11-29 |website=Le Point |language=fr}}</ref>

The [[British Council|British Council's]] Cultural Protection Fund funded a conservation programme at Tell Umm el-'Amr.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Preservation of archaeological sites in the Gaza strip |url=https://www.britishcouncil.org/partner/international-development/track-record/preservation-archaeological-sites-gaza-strip |access-date=25 November 2024 |website=British Council}}</ref>

In 2024, a request for UNESCO world heritage status was submitted.<ref name="UNESCO2024">{{Cite web |title=Nominations to be examined at the 46th session of the World Heritage Committee (2024) |url=https://whc.unesco.org/en/newproperties/ |access-date=2024-07-02 |website=UNESCO World Heritage Convention |language=en |archive-date=2021-07-31 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210731170733/https://whc.unesco.org/en/newproperties/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Following their addition in July 2024, both Tell Umm el-'Amr and Saint Hilarion Monastery were included on the [[List of World Heritage in Danger]] by UNESCO.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2024-07-26 |title=The Monastery of Saint Hilarion/Tell Umm Amer in Palestine is inscribed on the List of World Heritage in Danger |url=https://whc.unesco.org/en/news/2704/ |access-date=2024-07-26 |website=UNESCO World Heritage Centre |language=en |archive-date=2024-07-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240730084058/https://whc.unesco.org/en/news/2704/ |url-status=live }}</ref>
