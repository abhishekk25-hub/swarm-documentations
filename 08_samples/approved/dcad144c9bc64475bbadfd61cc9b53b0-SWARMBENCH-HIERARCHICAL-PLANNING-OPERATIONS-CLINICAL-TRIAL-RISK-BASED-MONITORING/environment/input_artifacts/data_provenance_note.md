DATA PROVENANCE NOTE -- quarterly risk-based monitoring plan
Contract research organization clinical-operations portfolio (cardiometabolic / type 2 diabetes program)

trials/NCT*.txt
  One human-readable study record per trial in the portfolio. These are real public
  records retrieved verbatim from ClinicalTrials.gov, the U.S. National Library of
  Medicine clinical-studies registry (Data API v2, https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2;
  records are U.S. Government public-domain data, 17 U.S.C. 105). Each record carries
  the trial's title, sponsor, overall recruitment status, study type and phase,
  enrollment (count and basis), facility locations and their site-level statuses,
  interventions, eligibility and the last-update date. Formats vary between records as
  they do in the registry; the monitoring-risk fields must be read from each record.

monitoring_rulebook.md
  The CRO's adopted risk-based-monitoring triage method (in-scope determination,
  status reconciliation, risk score and tiers, monitoring hours, visit-window cadence,
  CRA capacity and the reference scheduling order). It is the binding method of record
  and is grounded in ICH E6(R2), the FDA risk-based-monitoring guidance and the
  TransCelerate RBM framework. The monitoring-hour capacity, the risk-point weights and
  the visit-window cadence are this CRO's stated operating parameters.

sources_to_confirm.md
  The authorities quality and regulatory require confirmed against the official source
  and cited with a frozen retrieval date.
