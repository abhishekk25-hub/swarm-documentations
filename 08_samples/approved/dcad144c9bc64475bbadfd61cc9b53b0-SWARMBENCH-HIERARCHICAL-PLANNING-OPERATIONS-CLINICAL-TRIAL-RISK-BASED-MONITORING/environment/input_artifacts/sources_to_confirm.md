CONTRACT RESEARCH ORGANIZATION -- CLINICAL OPERATIONS
AUTHORITIES AND DATA SOURCES TO CONFIRM AND CITE

Quality and regulatory require every monitoring-planning cycle to confirm the
following authorities against the live/official source, record the URL actually
retrieved and the retrieval date, and note the specific requirement or fact each
supplies. Use the official source for each (secondary summaries are not accepted).

S-01 | ICH E6(R2) Good Clinical Practice
      what it supplies: the GCP standard and the risk-based approach to monitoring (section 5.0 / 5.18)
      official source: ich.org
S-02 | FDA Guidance: Oversight of Clinical Investigations -- A Risk-Based Approach to Monitoring
      what it supplies: the FDA expectation for risk-based monitoring of clinical investigations
      official source: fda.gov
S-03 | ICH E8(R1) General Considerations for Clinical Studies
      what it supplies: quality-by-design and critical-to-quality factors that drive monitoring focus
      official source: ich.org
S-04 | 21 CFR Part 312 -- Investigational New Drug Application
      what it supplies: the IND framework governing monitoring of regulated drug/biologic trials
      official source: ecfr.gov
S-05 | 21 CFR Part 812 -- Investigational Device Exemptions
      what it supplies: the IDE framework governing monitoring of regulated device trials
      official source: ecfr.gov
S-06 | 21 CFR Part 11 -- Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures
      what it supplies: electronic-records integrity expectations for monitored trial data
      official source: ecfr.gov
S-07 | FDAAA 801 / 42 CFR Part 11 -- ClinicalTrials.gov registration and results
      what it supplies: the registration/results-reporting obligations tied to the portfolio records
      official source: clinicaltrials.gov
S-08 | 45 CFR Part 46 -- Protection of Human Subjects (Common Rule)
      what it supplies: the human-subjects protection basis underlying site oversight
      official source: hhs.gov
S-09 | TransCelerate BioPharma Risk-Based Monitoring framework
      what it supplies: the industry RBM methodology the rulebook's risk tiers are normalized to
      official source: transceleratebiopharmainc.com
S-10 | FDA Guidance: A Risk-Based Approach to Monitoring of Clinical Investigations -- Questions and Answers
      what it supplies: operational Q&A clarifying monitoring plan content and frequency
      official source: fda.gov
S-11 | NLM ClinicalTrials.gov -- registry data and field definitions
      what it supplies: the provenance and field definitions for the portfolio's source records
      official source: clinicaltrials.gov
S-12 | ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice
      what it supplies: the current GCP revision modernizing risk-based quality management
      official source: ich.org
