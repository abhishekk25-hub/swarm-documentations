A few things I already know are going to bite if you are not careful, because they bit us last cycle. The scheduling feed and the adopted proposal do not agree on every route - on some routes the GTFS trip counts are stale - and our policy is clear that the adopted proposal governs; do not quietly use whichever number you read first, and log every place they disagree. Our policy evaluates concurrently proposed changes in the aggregate, so the equity rollup is the whole package, not just the routes that individually clear the twenty-five percent major-change line; whether each smaller change is in the rollup follows from the policy, not from whether it is individually major. And a couple of routes have walksheds that barely overlap any populated block groups - the policy says to set those aside for the equity math and disclose why, rather than feed unreliable demographic shares into the totals.
