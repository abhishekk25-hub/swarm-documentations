COUNTY CONNECT TRANSIT AUTHORITY -- FY2026 TITLE VI ANALYSIS
AUTHORITIES AND DATA SOURCES TO CONFIRM AND CITE

Counsel requires every analysis to confirm the following authorities and data
sources against the live/official source, record the URL actually retrieved and
the retrieval date, and note the specific requirement or fact each one supplies.
Use the official source for each (the agency does not accept secondary summaries).

S-01 | FTA Circular 4702.1B (Title VI Requirements and Guidelines for FTA Recipients)
      what it supplies: service-equity analysis requirements; minority and low-income definitions
      official source: transit.dot.gov
S-02 | Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. 2000d)
      what it supplies: statutory prohibition on discrimination in federally assisted programs
      official source: govinfo.gov
S-03 | 49 CFR Part 21 (DOT Title VI implementing regulations)
      what it supplies: DOT nondiscrimination regulations binding on FTA recipients
      official source: ecfr.gov
S-04 | DOT Order 5610.2C (Environmental Justice)
      what it supplies: DOT environmental-justice policy on low-income and minority populations
      official source: transportation.gov
S-05 | Executive Order 12898 (Environmental Justice)
      what it supplies: federal EJ mandate underlying DOT/FTA equity review
      official source: federalregister.gov
S-06 | ACS Table B03002 (Hispanic or Latino Origin by Race)
      what it supplies: source table for the minority population share
      official source: data.census.gov
S-07 | ACS Table C17002 (Ratio of Income to Poverty Level)
      what it supplies: source table for the low-income population share
      official source: data.census.gov
S-08 | ACS Table B08201 (Household Size by Vehicles Available)
      what it supplies: zero-vehicle households / transit dependence context
      official source: data.census.gov
S-09 | ACS Table C16001 (Language Spoken at Home)
      what it supplies: limited-English-proficiency population context
      official source: data.census.gov
S-10 | ACS Table B01001 (Sex by Age)
      what it supplies: older-adult ridership context
      official source: data.census.gov
S-11 | FTA National Transit Database (NTD)
      what it supplies: reporting framework / service-level reference
      official source: transit.dot.gov
S-12 | GTFS Reference (General Transit Feed Specification)
      what it supplies: schedule-feed format behind the GTFS export
      official source: gtfs.org
S-13 | U.S. Census Bureau ACS margin-of-error / methodology guidance
      what it supplies: reliability/MOE caveats on the demographic shares
      official source: census.gov
