DATA PROVENANCE NOTE -- FY2026 Title VI service-equity analysis inputs
County Connect Transit Authority -- fixed-route network, central Contra Costa County, California

service_change_package_fy2026.csv
  The adopted FY2026 service-change package (proposal of record). Before/after daily revenue
  miles and daily trips by affected day type, from the Authority's FY2026 service plan. This
  file governs planned service levels.

gtfs_schedule_export.csv
  Post-change scheduled trip counts pulled from the draft GTFS feed (feed_version
  fy2026_draft_v3). A working export, not the adopted proposal; may lag the proposal on
  individual routes. GTFS follows the General Transit Feed Specification
  (https://gtfs.org/documentation/schedule/reference/).

walkshed_demographics_acs.csv
  1/4-mile route walkshed populations with minority and low-income shares from the U.S. Census
  Bureau American Community Survey 2018-2022 5-year estimates -- table B03002 (Hispanic or Latino
  origin by race) for minority share and C17002 (ratio of income to poverty level in the past 12
  months) for low-income share -- apportioned to the walksheds at the block-group level by the
  Authority's GIS staff. acs_blockgroup_overlap_pct is the share of each walkshed population drawn
  from block groups with adequate ACS coverage. Source tables:
  https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2022.B03002 and
  https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2022.C17002.

service_calendar_fy2026.csv
  Authority FY2026 service calendar (annual service days by day type).

title_vi_equity_policy.md
  The Authority's adopted Title VI Major Service Change / Disparate Impact / Disproportionate
  Burden policy (Board Resolution 2021-14, as amended), consistent with FTA Circular 4702.1B.
  Binding method of record.

public_comment_log.txt
  Verbatim public-comment excerpts (comment_id | channel | topic | comment) from the FY2026
  hearings and online portal. Every comment must be addressed with a disposition.

sources_to_confirm.md
  The authorities and ACS data tables counsel requires confirmed against the official source
  and cited with a frozen retrieval date.
