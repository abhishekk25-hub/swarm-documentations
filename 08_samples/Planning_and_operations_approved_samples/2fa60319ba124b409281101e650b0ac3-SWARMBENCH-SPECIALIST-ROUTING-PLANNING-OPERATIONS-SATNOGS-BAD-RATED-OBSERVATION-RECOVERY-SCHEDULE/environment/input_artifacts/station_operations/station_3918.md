# Station 3918 - YL3CT-1

- Ground station ID: 3918
- Location: 56.857, 24.709 at 45 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/3918/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a pair of crossed yagis into an RTL-SDR Blog v3 behind a filtered preamp, driven from an old ThinkPad in the garage. The shelter was rebuilt in January to keep damp out which suits the unattended operating we do here.

## Recent operating history

The station logged around 580 observations last month which is worth knowing when you plan around us.

The 32 degree elevation floor this site used to run was lifted in March once the obstruction came down, so only the standard network minimum applies now.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Job intake is manual-review though back-to-back passes on opposite azimuths can lose a few seconds.

An unplanned outage from 2026-06-23T11:00:00Z to 2026-06-24T13:00:00Z took the site down when the network link failed, now fully resolved and with no bearing on the current recovery window.

## Availability for the recovery window

We would ask you to route FSK AX.100 Mode 5 to another site; sample rates for it exceed what the host can sustain. Sorry to add to the workload.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The feed is a hairpin match with a measured VSWR under 1.3 which suits our mostly-overhead traffic.

## Software and configuration

The site runs the stock Raspbian image pinned to a known-good release so behaviour is predictable between updates.

## Neighbouring coverage

Our footprint overlaps 5 neighbours to the east so a gap here is a genuine gap in coverage.

## Typical traffic

Roughly 80 per cent of our passes are UHF so unusual modes occasionally surprise us.

## Staffing

Two members handle maintenance between them and handover notes are kept on the club wiki.

## Power and connectivity

The site runs off the building's landlord supply with 40 minutes of battery behind it which has ridden out every cut so far this year. The site uses fixed wireless with a fixed address though the monthly allowance is not generous.

## Local interference

We see intermittent interference around 172 MHz from a neighbour and a notch filter has largely dealt with it.

## Calibration

Rotator alignment was re-surveyed in March which keeps Doppler correction honest.

## Fault handling

Alerts route to the site owner first, then the club which has kept downtime short this year.

## Data quality

Recordings are archived locally for 45 days and uploaded on completion which we are slowly working to improve. The receiver drifts by about 4 ppm between GPS corrections which we are slowly working to improve.

## Other remarks

A 2-booking weekly limit trialled during the March campaign to manage upload costs finished with it, and no booking limit is in force for this recovery window.
