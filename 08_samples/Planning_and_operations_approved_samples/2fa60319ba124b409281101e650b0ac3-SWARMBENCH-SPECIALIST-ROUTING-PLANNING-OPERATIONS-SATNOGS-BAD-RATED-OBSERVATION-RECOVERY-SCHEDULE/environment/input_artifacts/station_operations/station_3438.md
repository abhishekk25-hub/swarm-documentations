# Station 3438 - StreetDirt UHF

- Ground station ID: 3438
- Location: 52.21, 5.16 at 14 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/3438/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a stacked dipole array into an RTL-SDR v4 behind a switched attenuator for strong passes, driven from a rack-mounted NUC. The site has been in the network since 2021 so the configuration has been stable for a while.

## Recent operating history

This site mostly serves the eastern horizon which is worth knowing when you plan around us.

The Wednesday-morning routine maintenance we once ran between 0900Z and 1000Z ended in February and no longer applies.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

The station accepts bookings up to 7 days ahead so the published queue is what actually flies.

A 3-booking weekly limit trialled during the March campaign to manage upload costs finished with it, and no booking limit is in force for this recovery window.

## Availability for the recovery window

Please note: count on 1 slots from us, not more; the site shares a link with the household and we must be fair to it. Sorry to add to the workload. Before anything is booked, there is a hard blackout on this site from 13:45 UTC on the 22nd to 21:45 UTC on the 22nd; the dish drive is being re-greased and re-aligned. Normal service continues alongside. An earlier revision of this note listed a booking freeze, lifted at 2026-07-08T12:00:00Z after the feedline was signed off, since when we have accepted work normally.

## Antenna and rotator detail

Elevation is fixed and the array is trimmed to 37 degrees and pointing has held true since.

## Software and configuration

Scheduling is driven by a Docker deployment with local patches though it does mean new features arrive late here.

## Neighbouring coverage

There is no other coverage within about 208 km so a gap here is a genuine gap in coverage.

## Typical traffic

Our operators favour VHF targets so unusual modes occasionally surprise us.

## Staffing

A rota of 4 keyholders shares the work though nobody is on site during the week.

## Power and connectivity

Power comes from the club's metered feed, backed by a 2 kW inverter and consumption has been stable since the rebuild. The station has a dedicated a campus link line and transfers finish well inside the pass gap.

## Local interference

We see intermittent interference around 274 MHz from a neighbour which the operator re-checks each quarter.

## Calibration

We check frequency alignment monthly against a known beacon and nothing has moved since.

## Fault handling

The operator is paged automatically on three consecutive failures though weekends can run to a day or two.

## Data quality

Decoder success here has run around 93 per cent over the past year which we are slowly working to improve. Frame decoding is handled downstream rather than on site which we are slowly working to improve.

## Other remarks

An unplanned outage from 2026-06-17T11:00:00Z to 2026-06-17T17:00:00Z took the site down when the network link failed, closed out at the time and with no bearing on the current recovery window.
