# Station 3106 - DEX-ONE

- Ground station ID: 3106
- Location: 50.469, 5.718 at 162 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/3106/
- Note revised: 2026-07-20
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a pair of crossed yagis into a PlutoSDR behind a filtered preamp, driven from an Odroid in the loft. Weather exposure is the main limiting factor here though winter still costs us a handful of passes.

## Recent operating history

Volunteers rebuilt the feed arrangement in June and the pattern has been consistent.

A tentative plan to embargo LRPT work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before October and every mode accepted meanwhile.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

The station accepts bookings up to 14 days ahead and the rotator pre-positions a little ahead of AOS.

Our sister site 3136 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Availability for the recovery window

Ahead of the 22-25 July window, please keep FSK observations off this station; there is a decoding fault we have not traced yet. Other than that we are fully available. Worth flagging before you schedule: we have planned maintenance from 1945Z on the 24th until 1345Z on the 25th; cabling is being re-run through the new duct. Other than that we are fully available.

## Antenna and rotator detail

Elevation is fixed and the array is trimmed to 39 degrees so slew time between passes is around 21 seconds.

## Software and configuration

We track the upstream client but hold back one minor version which has avoided the regressions others hit.

## Neighbouring coverage

The regional group meets monthly to divide the load so handing work over is usually straightforward.

## Typical traffic

We see mainly S-band amateur payloads with the rest spread across other bands.

## Staffing

The site is unattended and checked remotely which keeps the workload manageable.

## Power and connectivity

The site runs off the building's landlord supply with two hours of battery behind it which has ridden out every cut so far this year. We backhaul over a campus link from the mast to the house and latency has never affected scheduling.

## Local interference

A 242 MHz carrier appears most weekday afternoons so we schedule around it where we can.

## Calibration

We check frequency alignment monthly against a known beacon and nothing has moved since.

## Fault handling

Anything unusual is logged and reviewed weekly and the log is public on request.

## Data quality

The host reboots for updates at 0300Z which we are slowly working to improve. Frame decoding is handled downstream rather than on site so treat marginal passes with a little caution.

## Other remarks

The Friday-morning routine maintenance we once ran between 0600Z and 1300Z ended in March and no longer applies.

## Contact

Reach the operator of station 3106 on the community forum and we are happy to discuss alternatives.
