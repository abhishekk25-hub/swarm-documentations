# Station 4497 - VA3HEV

- Ground station ID: 4497
- Location: 44.255731, -76.38565 at 110 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/4497/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a modified Wimo X-Quad into an Airspy R2 behind an inline SAW filter, driven from a Pi 5 with an SSD. This site replaced an older installation a few streets away with no changes planned before the autumn.

## Recent operating history

Traffic through this site has been steady all summer though we do review anything anomalous.

Our sister site 4512 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Bookings are confirmed back to the network within 15 minutes which keeps broken or duplicated requests off the air.

A tentative plan to embargo BPSK work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before August and every mode accepted meanwhile.

## Availability for the recovery window

While the catch-up runs, we cannot accept any recovery bookings between 22 and 25 July; the new feedline is still awaiting certification. We remain open either side of it.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The rotator is a home-built stepper drive driven over an Ethernet bridge though it wants re-checking each spring.

## Software and configuration

The site runs satnogs-client 1.9 pinned to a known-good release so a rebuild takes under an hour.

## Neighbouring coverage

The nearest other station is roughly 26 km away which matters when we go offline.

## Typical traffic

Most of our traffic is VHF weather and cubesat work and the hardware is tuned for that.

## Staffing

A rota of 4 keyholders shares the work so response outside evenings can be slow.

## Power and connectivity

The rack draws about 51 W continuous from a domestic single-phase supply which has ridden out every cut so far this year. Uplink is a campus link shared with the household though the monthly allowance is not generous.

## Local interference

A 297 MHz carrier appears most weekday afternoons which the operator re-checks each quarter.

## Calibration

Gain figures were re-measured after the June rebuild with results filed on the station page.

## Fault handling

Anything unusual is logged and reviewed weekly which has kept downtime short this year.

## Data quality

Decoder success here has run around 99 per cent over the past year and the effect is easy to spot on the waterfall. Waterfall uploads occasionally stall on the domestic link and downstream products are unaffected.
