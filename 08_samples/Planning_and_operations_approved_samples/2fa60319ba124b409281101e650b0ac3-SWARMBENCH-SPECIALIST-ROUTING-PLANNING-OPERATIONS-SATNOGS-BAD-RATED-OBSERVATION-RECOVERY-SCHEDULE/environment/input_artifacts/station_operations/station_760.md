# Station 760 - KD5QZG - VHF/UHF

- Ground station ID: 760
- Location: 30.179, -92.054 at 11 m AMSL

- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a homebrew turnstile into an Airspy Mini behind a helical bandpass filter, driven from a rack-mounted NUC. The mast was re-aligned in March after a storm which suits the unattended operating we do here.

## Recent operating history

Operating hours here are effectively unattended which is worth knowing when you plan around us.

Our sister site 764 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Bookings are confirmed back to the network within 15 minutes with a short grace period for cancellations.

A tentative plan to embargo DUV work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before August and every mode accepted meanwhile.

## Availability for the recovery window

One constraint from our side: count on 1 slots from us, not more; we are on a metered connection this month. Apologies for the inconvenience.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The rotator is a ARSWIN controller driven over GPIO from the host though it wants re-checking each spring.

## Software and configuration

The host image was rebuilt in March onto a Docker deployment so behaviour is predictable between updates.

## Neighbouring coverage

The nearest other station is roughly 136 km away though we do not formally share a queue.

## Typical traffic

Roughly 59 per cent of our passes are S-band and results there are consistently good.

## Staffing

The site is unattended and checked remotely with the club providing cover during holidays.

## Power and connectivity

Power comes from a rooftop solar array with grid tie, backed by a 3 kW inverter so a grid dip usually costs us a pass or two. We backhaul over fixed wireless from the mast to the house which occasionally drops during heavy weather.

## Local interference

A 276 MHz carrier appears most weekday afternoons and it is documented in our station notes upstream.

## Calibration

The chain was swept end to end in June and drift since has been within tolerance.

## Fault handling

Alerts route to the site owner first, then the club with a monthly summary to the network.

## Data quality

The host reboots for updates at 0400Z so treat marginal passes with a little caution. The host reboots for updates at 0100Z which rarely defeats the decoder outright.
