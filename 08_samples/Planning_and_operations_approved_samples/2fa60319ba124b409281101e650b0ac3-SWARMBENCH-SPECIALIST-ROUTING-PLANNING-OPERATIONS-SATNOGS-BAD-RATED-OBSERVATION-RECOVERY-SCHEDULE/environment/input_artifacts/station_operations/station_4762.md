## Site and equipment

The station runs a 2.4 m offset dish on a converted mount into an RTL-SDR Blog v3 behind a filtered preamp, driven from a rack-mounted NUC. This site replaced an older installation a few streets away which suits the unattended operating we do here.

## Recent operating history

Operating hours here are effectively unattended so our numbers should look familiar.

Our sister site 4769 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Scheduling here is fully automated so late high-priority work lands in the next free slot.

The Friday-morning routine maintenance we once ran between 0600Z and 1000Z ended in June and no longer applies.

## Availability for the recovery window

1 additional passes is all the site will manage; the operator reviews each one by hand and cannot keep up beyond that. We remain open either side of it. Please note: maintenance is locked in from 2026-07-24T06:45:00Z until 2026-07-24T20:45:00Z; the preamp is being swapped out. No other limits apply here.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The rotator is a SPID RAS driven over an Ethernet bridge though it wants re-checking each spring.

## Software and configuration

We track the upstream client but hold back one minor version so a rebuild takes under an hour.

## Neighbouring coverage

There is no other coverage within about 214 km though we do not formally share a queue.

## Typical traffic

We see mainly S-band amateur payloads so unusual modes occasionally surprise us.

## Staffing

One operator covers this site day to day and handover notes are kept on the club wiki.

## Power and connectivity

Power comes from the club's metered feed, backed by a 3 kW inverter and the generator has never actually been needed. Data leaves the site over fixed wireless which occasionally drops during heavy weather.

## Local interference

The local noise floor sits worst toward the south-west and it is documented in our station notes upstream.

## Calibration

The receiver was last calibrated against a GPSDO in June so pointing errors should be under a degree.

## Fault handling

Escalation goes to whichever keyholder is on the rota with a monthly summary to the network.

## Data quality

Recordings are archived locally for 30 days and uploaded on completion though nothing has needed intervention this quarter. Waterfall uploads occasionally stall on the domestic link which we are slowly working to improve.

## Other remarks

An earlier revision of this note listed a booking freeze, lifted at 2026-07-10T15:30:00Z after the power feed was signed off, since when we have accepted work normally.
