# Station 4186 - IAR-GS

- Ground station ID: 4186
- Location: -34.8663, -58.139 at 10 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/4186/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a twin-band vertical with a phasing harness into a HackRF One behind an inline SAW filter, driven from an old ThinkPad in the garage. This site replaced an older installation a few streets away and it has needed very little attention since.

## Recent operating history

Traffic through this site has been steady all summer though we do review anything anomalous.

A tentative plan to embargo LRPT work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before August and every mode accepted meanwhile.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Job intake is manual-review so late high-priority work lands in the next free slot.

Our sister site 4201 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Availability for the recovery window

Nothing to flag from our side for 22-25 July. Happy to take whatever you send.

## Antenna and rotator detail

Elevation is fixed and the array is trimmed to 20 degrees which suits our mostly-overhead traffic.

## Software and configuration

Scheduling is driven by satnogs-client 1.9 with local patches which has avoided the regressions others hit.

## Neighbouring coverage

There is no other coverage within about 151 km so a gap here is a genuine gap in coverage.

## Typical traffic

Roughly 79 per cent of our passes are VHF with the rest spread across other bands.

## Staffing

The station is run by 3 volunteers with the club providing cover during holidays.

## Power and connectivity

Power comes from a rooftop solar array with grid tie, backed by a 2 kW inverter and the generator has never actually been needed. The site uses ADSL with a fixed address so we compress artefacts before sending them.

## Local interference

Pager traffic near 462 MHz used to swamp us so we schedule around it where we can.

## Calibration

Rotator alignment was re-surveyed in May which keeps Doppler correction honest.

## Fault handling

Escalation goes to whichever keyholder is on the rota and response is usually the same evening.

## Data quality

Decoder success here has run around 97 per cent over the past year which we are slowly working to improve. Recordings are archived locally for 14 days and uploaded on completion and downstream products are unaffected.

## Other remarks

An earlier revision of this note listed a booking freeze, lifted at 2026-07-20T10:00:00Z after the power feed was signed off, since when we have accepted work normally.
