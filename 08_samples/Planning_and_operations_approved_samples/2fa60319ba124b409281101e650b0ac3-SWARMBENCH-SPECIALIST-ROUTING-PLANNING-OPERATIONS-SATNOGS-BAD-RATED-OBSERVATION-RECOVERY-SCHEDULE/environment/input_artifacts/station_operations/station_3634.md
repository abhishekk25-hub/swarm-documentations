# Station 3634 - ZR6DM @ Heidelberg - UHF

- Ground station ID: 3634
- Location: -26.518, 28.296 at 1600 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/3634/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a tape-measure yagi on a hand-built azimuth drive into a HackRF One behind a filtered preamp, driven from a Pi 5 with an SSD. Uptime last quarter ran at about 89 per cent and the logs have been quiet ever since.

## Recent operating history

Traffic through this site has been steady all summer and nothing about that changes for the catch-up.

An earlier revision of this note listed a booking freeze, lifted at 2026-07-13T07:45:00Z after the power feed was signed off, since when we have accepted work normally.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

We run a conservative queue depth of about 20 and the operator clears anything stuck by hand most evenings.

An unplanned outage from 2026-07-07T15:00:00Z to 2026-07-07T21:00:00Z took the site down when the rotator controller failed, and the site has been stable since and with no bearing on the current recovery window.

## Availability for the recovery window

Ahead of the 22-25 July window, the site is only worth using above 33 degrees peak; low-angle multipath here is severe. Sorry to add to the workload. We will take 1 and decline anything beyond that; the operator reviews each one by hand and cannot keep up beyond that. Other than that we are fully available. Just so the desk knows, please keep FSK AX.100 Mode 5 observations off this station; that path is being re-cabled this week. That is the only limit from us.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The feed is a hairpin match with a measured VSWR under 1.8 and loss over that run is about 0.8 dB.

## Software and configuration

We track the upstream client but hold back one minor version which has avoided the regressions others hit.

## Neighbouring coverage

This site sits inside a cluster of 2 stations so a gap here is a genuine gap in coverage.

## Typical traffic

Most of our traffic is S-band weather and cubesat work though we take whatever the network sends.

## Staffing

Two members handle maintenance between them and escalation is documented on the network page.

## Power and connectivity

The rack draws about 74 W continuous from a rooftop solar array with grid tie and the generator has never actually been needed. The station has a dedicated fixed wireless line so large waterfall uploads queue up overnight.

## Local interference

A 462 MHz carrier appears most weekday afternoons and a notch filter has largely dealt with it.

## Calibration

The chain was swept end to end in April and nothing has moved since.

## Fault handling

Faults are raised through the club mailing list though weekends can run to a day or two.

## Data quality

Recordings are archived locally for 30 days and uploaded on completion so the occasional pass gets clipped. Baseline noise sits about 4 dB above the network median so treat marginal passes with a little caution.

## Other remarks

A maintenance slot floated for 24 July at 12:00Z was cancelled when the contractor fell through, pushing the work to September and leaving the station up throughout this window.

## Contact

Station 3634 is reachable through the usual operator channels and we are happy to discuss alternatives.
