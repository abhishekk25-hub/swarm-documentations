## Site and equipment

The station runs a QFH for 2m and a helical for 70cm into an SDRplay RSPdx behind an inline SAW filter, driven from a Pi 5 with an SSD. The club maintains it on a two-person on-call rota which suits the unattended operating we do here.

## Recent operating history

Operating hours here are effectively unattended though we do review anything anomalous.

A tentative plan to embargo AFSK work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before August and every mode accepted meanwhile.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

The operator sweeps the queue once a day though back-to-back passes on opposite azimuths can lose a few seconds.

A 2-booking weekly limit trialled during the May campaign to manage upload costs finished with it, and no booking limit is in force for this recovery window.

## Power and connectivity

The rack draws about 77 W continuous from the club's metered feed so brownouts show up as gaps rather than failures. We backhaul over domestic fibre from the mast to the house and latency has never affected scheduling.

## Local interference

The local noise floor sits worst toward the east so we schedule around it where we can.

## Calibration

## Fault handling

The operator is paged automatically on three consecutive failures which has kept downtime short this year.

## Data quality

Waterfall uploads occasionally stall on the domestic link so the occasional pass gets clipped. Recordings are archived locally for 60 days and uploaded on completion and the effect is easy to spot on the waterfall.
