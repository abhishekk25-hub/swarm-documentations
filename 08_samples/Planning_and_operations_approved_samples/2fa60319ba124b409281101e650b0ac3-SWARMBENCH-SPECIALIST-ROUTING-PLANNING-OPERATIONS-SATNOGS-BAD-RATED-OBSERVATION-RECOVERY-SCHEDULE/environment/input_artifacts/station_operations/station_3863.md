# Station 3863 - VK2RZ

- Ground station ID: 3863
- Location: -33.729, 151.264 at 90 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/3863/
- Note revised: 2026-07-20
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a QFH for 2m and a helical for 70cm into a PlutoSDR behind a helical bandpass filter, driven from an Odroid in the loft. Weather exposure is the main limiting factor here and it has needed very little attention since.

## Recent operating history

Volunteers rebuilt the feed arrangement in June so expect the usual throughput.

A 2-booking weekly limit trialled during the June campaign to manage upload costs finished with it, and no booking limit is in force for this recovery window.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Bookings are confirmed back to the network within 10 minutes so the published queue is what actually flies.

An earlier revision of this note listed a booking freeze, lifted at 2026-07-08T15:00:00Z after the host was signed off, since when we have accepted work normally.

## Availability for the recovery window

Station 3863 is open for the whole 22-25 July period. We have capacity to spare this week.

## Antenna and rotator detail

Elevation is fixed and the array is trimmed to 59 degrees and loss over that run is about 1.6 dB.

## Software and configuration

The host image was rebuilt in June onto satnogs-client 1.9 though it does mean new features arrive late here.

## Neighbouring coverage

Our footprint overlaps 6 neighbours to the north and the split has worked well so far.

## Typical traffic

Historically this site has specialised in S-band work though we take whatever the network sends.

## Staffing

The site is unattended and checked remotely and handover notes are kept on the club wiki.

## Power and connectivity

A 3 kW supply feeds the shelter through an isolating transformer and consumption has been stable since the rebuild. We backhaul over a campus link from the mast to the house and latency has never affected scheduling.

## Local interference

The local noise floor sits worst toward the north which mostly affects the weaker downlinks.

## Calibration

Gain figures were re-measured after the January rebuild and drift since has been within tolerance.

## Fault handling

Alerts route to the site owner first, then the club though weekends can run to a day or two.

## Data quality

Waterfall uploads occasionally stall on the domestic link so raw audio is the better source if in doubt. Waterfall uploads occasionally stall on the domestic link and the effect is easy to spot on the waterfall.
