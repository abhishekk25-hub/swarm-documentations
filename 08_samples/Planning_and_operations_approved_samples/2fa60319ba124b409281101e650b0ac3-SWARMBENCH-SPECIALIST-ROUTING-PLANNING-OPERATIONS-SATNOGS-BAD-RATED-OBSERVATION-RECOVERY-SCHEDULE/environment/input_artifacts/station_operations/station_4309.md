## Site and equipment

The station runs a fixed zenith turnstile into a HackRF One behind an inline SAW filter, driven from a rack-mounted NUC. The shelter was rebuilt in March to keep damp out with no changes planned before the autumn.

## Power and connectivity

A 3 kW supply feeds the shelter through an isolating transformer and the generator has never actually been needed. The site uses a campus link with a fixed address and transfers finish well inside the pass gap.

## Local interference
