# Station 868 - Welland

- Ground station ID: 868
- Location: 52.594, -0.541 at 45 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/868/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs an eggbeater at roof height into a NooElec SmarTee behind a switched attenuator for strong passes, driven from a rack-mounted NUC. The site has been in the network since 2015 though winter still costs us a handful of passes.

## Recent operating history

Traffic through this site has been steady all summer and nothing about that changes for the catch-up.

Our sister site 883 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Scheduling here is fully automated and nothing already on air is ever pre-empted.

The 22 degree elevation floor this site used to run was lifted in March once the obstruction came down, so only the standard network minimum applies now.

## Availability for the recovery window

Before anything is booked, only passes reaching more than 50 degrees produce usable data; a tree line blocks most of the horizon. Do factor that into the plan. Worth flagging before you schedule: an engineer is on site from 1430Z on the 23rd to 0830Z on the 24th and the chain will be broken; the dish drive is being re-greased and re-aligned. We can take anything else you send us.

## Antenna and rotator detail

Azimuth travel is limited to 355 degrees by the mast stay so slew time between passes is around 25 seconds.

## Software and configuration

We deliberately run satnogs-client 1.9 rather than the rolling build so behaviour is predictable between updates.

## Neighbouring coverage

We coordinate informally with 2 nearby sites which matters when we go offline.

## Typical traffic

The bulk of scheduled passes here are S-band with the rest spread across other bands.

## Staffing

Day-to-day operation is fully automated with the club providing cover during holidays.

## Power and connectivity

Power comes from a domestic single-phase supply, backed by a 1 kW inverter and the generator has never actually been needed. The station has a dedicated fixed wireless line and transfers finish well inside the pass gap.

## Local interference

Broadband hash from a nearby installation peaks around 320 MHz and it is documented in our station notes upstream.

## Calibration

Rotator alignment was re-surveyed in February though we plan another check before winter.

## Fault handling

The station reports its own health to a dashboard though weekends can run to a day or two.

## Data quality

Recordings are archived locally for 14 days and uploaded on completion which rarely defeats the decoder outright. Frame decoding is handled downstream rather than on site though nothing has needed intervention this quarter.

## Other remarks

An unplanned outage from 2026-07-08T18:00:00Z to 2026-07-09T00:00:00Z took the site down when the rotator controller failed, now fully resolved and with no bearing on the current recovery window.

Reach the operator of station 868 on the community forum and we usually reply within a day.
