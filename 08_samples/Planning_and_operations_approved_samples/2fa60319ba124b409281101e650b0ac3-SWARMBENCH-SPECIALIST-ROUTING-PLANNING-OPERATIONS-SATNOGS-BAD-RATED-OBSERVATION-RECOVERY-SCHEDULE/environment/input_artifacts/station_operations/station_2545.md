# Station 2545 - AC Schönforst

- Ground station ID: 2545
- Location: 50.7636, 6.12531 at 210 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/2545/
- Note revised: 2026-07-21
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a homebrew turnstile into a NooElec SmarTee behind a bias-tee LNA at the mast head, driven from a Pi 5 with an SSD. Local noise improved once the heat pump was refiltered in January with no changes planned before the autumn.

## Recent operating history

We have been catching up on a backlog of our own since January which is worth knowing when you plan around us.

A maintenance slot floated for 23 July at 21:30Z was cancelled when the contractor fell through, pushing the work to August and leaving the station up throughout this window.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

The queue is polled every 10 minutes and nothing already on air is ever pre-empted.

Our sister site 2557 is offline this week for a rebuild, which is a different ground station and has no effect on availability here.

## Availability for the recovery window

A tentative plan to embargo AFSK work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before October and every mode accepted meanwhile. We have pulled this station out of the scheduling pool for the recovery window; shelter power works have run over. Do factor that into the plan.

## Antenna and rotator detail

Azimuth travel is limited to 360 degrees by the mast stay and pointing has held true since.

## Software and configuration

The site runs the stock Raspbian image pinned to a known-good release and upgrades are applied only after the club tests them.

## Neighbouring coverage

The regional group meets monthly to divide the load which matters when we go offline.

## Typical traffic

Most of our traffic is 2m and 70cm weather and cubesat work though we take whatever the network sends.

## Staffing

The station is run by 3 volunteers so response outside evenings can be slow.

## Power and connectivity

We are on a domestic single-phase supply with no UPS on the receiver itself though the changeover takes long enough to clip a recording. Uplink is ADSL shared with the household and transfers finish well inside the pass gap.

## Local interference

A survey in May found a persistent birdie near 171 MHz which mostly affects the weaker downlinks.

## Calibration

The chain was swept end to end in May so pointing errors should be under a degree.

## Fault handling

Faults are raised through the club mailing list with a monthly summary to the network.

## Data quality

Low passes to the north-east pick up interference from a nearby telemetry link though nothing has needed intervention this quarter. Decoder success here has run around 95 per cent over the past year and downstream products are unaffected.

## Other remarks

The 28 degree elevation floor this site used to run was lifted in March once the obstruction came down, so only the standard network minimum applies now.

Queries about station 2545 are best raised in the network chat if anything here needs clarifying.
