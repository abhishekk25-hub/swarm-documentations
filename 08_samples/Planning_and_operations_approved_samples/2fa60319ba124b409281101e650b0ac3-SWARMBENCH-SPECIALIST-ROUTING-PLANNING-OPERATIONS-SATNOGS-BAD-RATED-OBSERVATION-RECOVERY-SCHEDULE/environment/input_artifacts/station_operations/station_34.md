# Station 34 - M0SZT

- Ground station ID: 34
- Location: 53.0027, -2.1794 at 110 m AMSL
- Network page: https://network.satnogs.org/stations/34/
- Note revised: 2026-07-19
- Applies to: 22-25 July 2026 recovery horizon

## Site and equipment

The station runs a modified Wimo X-Quad into an Airspy R2 behind a 20 dB mast-head amplifier, driven from an Odroid in the loft. Uptime last quarter ran at about 98 per cent and the logs have been quiet ever since.

## Recent operating history

We have been catching up on a backlog of our own since March which is worth knowing when you plan around us.

The 26 degree elevation floor this site used to run was lifted in June once the obstruction came down, so only the standard network minimum applies now.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

The station accepts bookings up to 14 days ahead though the overnight window is checked only once.

An unplanned outage from 2026-07-02T15:00:00Z to 2026-07-03T11:00:00Z took the site down when the network link failed, closed out at the time and with no bearing on the current recovery window.

## Availability for the recovery window

The site is in normal service for the entire recovery window. Book us as needed.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The whole assembly was re-tensioned after the March storms and loss over that run is about 0.8 dB.

## Software and configuration

Automation here is the stock Raspbian image plus a handful of cron jobs which has avoided the regressions others hit.

## Neighbouring coverage

Our footprint overlaps 6 neighbours to the west and duplicate scheduling is rare as a result.

## Typical traffic

Our operators favour UHF targets so unusual modes occasionally surprise us.

## Staffing

The station is run by 3 volunteers with the club providing cover during holidays.

## Power and connectivity

We are on the building's landlord supply with no UPS on the receiver itself and consumption has been stable since the rebuild. Uplink is ADSL shared with the household and transfers finish well inside the pass gap.

## Local interference

A survey in February found a persistent birdie near 439 MHz which the operator re-checks each quarter.

## Calibration

Gain figures were re-measured after the June rebuild which keeps Doppler correction honest.

## Fault handling

Faults are raised through the club mailing list though weekends can run to a day or two.

## Data quality

The receiver drifts by about 4 ppm between GPS corrections so raw audio is the better source if in doubt. Baseline noise sits about 3 dB above the network median though nothing has needed intervention this quarter.

## Other remarks

A tentative plan to embargo AFSK work later in the year is still only a plan, with nothing changing before August and every mode accepted meanwhile.

## Contact

Scheduling questions go to the owner through the station 34 profile page if anything here needs clarifying.
