## Site and equipment

The station runs a fixed zenith turnstile into an Airspy Mini behind a bias-tee LNA at the mast head, driven from an Odroid in the loft. The club maintains it on a two-person on-call rota and the logs have been quiet ever since.

## Recent operating history

We have been catching up on a backlog of our own since May so our numbers should look familiar.

An earlier revision of this note listed a booking freeze, lifted at 2026-07-18T14:00:00Z after the preamp was signed off, since when we have accepted work normally.

## Scheduling and queue behaviour

Scheduling here is fully automated and duplicate submissions are dropped automatically.

The Thursday-morning routine maintenance we once ran between 0800Z and 1300Z ended in February and no longer applies.

## Availability for the recovery window

While the catch-up runs, nothing at all can be flown here before July 25; the mast is down for structural repair. Sorry to add to the workload.

## Antenna and rotator detail

The rotator is a home-built stepper drive driven over GPIO from the host though it wants re-checking each spring.
