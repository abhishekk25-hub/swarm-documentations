Medicare: Claim Review Programs Could Be Improved with Additional Prepayment Reviews and Better Data.  GAO-16-394 . Washington, D.C.: April 13, 2016. 

Year recommendation made: 2016 

Recommendation: In order to better ensure proper Medicare payments and protect Medicare funds, CMS should seek legislative authority to allow the recovery auditors to conduct prepayment claim reviews. 

Actions needed: HHS disagreed with this recommendation, noting that CMS has other program integrity activities to prevent improper payments. As of February 2025, HHS had not taken steps to seek legislative authority to allow the recovery auditors to conduct prepayment claim reviews. We maintain that CMS should seek legislative authority since prepayment reviews better protect agency funds compared with post-payment reviews. Until CMS seeks this authority, it will be missing an opportunity to help identify improper payments before they are made. 

High-risk area:  Medicare Program & Improper Payments
