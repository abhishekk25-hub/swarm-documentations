# Carbon price claim CPC-2026-16

Scheme: Morocco industrial decarbonisation levy
Character of the charge: production levy
Jurisdiction: Morocco
Period the payment relates to: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30
Installations the claim is made for: INST-127, INST-128
Amount paid: 9,860,000 MAD

Attestation: attached, signed by N. Benjelloun, expert comptable, Casablanca, who confirms that the person attesting is independent of the operator and of any undertaking connected with it, and that the amount above was paid to the authority administering the scheme.

Rebate: the operator states that no rebate, refund or other form of compensation was received for the amount claimed.

The claim is lodged against the consignments released for free circulation in the Union in the first half of 2026 that were produced at the installations named above.

Lodged with Scheldt Quay Customs Services NV on 31 July 2026.

## Basis of the charge

Liability under the scheme arises on the operator of the installation and cannot be passed to the purchaser of the goods.

The amount relates to the compliance obligation of the installations named and not to any obligation of other companies in the group.
