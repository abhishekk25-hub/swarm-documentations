# Carbon price claim CPC-2026-09

Scheme: Turkiye emissions trading system pilot phase
Character of the charge: emissions trading system
Jurisdiction: Turkiye
Period the payment relates to: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30
Installations the claim is made for: INST-118, INST-122
Amount paid: 3,260,000 TRY

Attestation: none is attached. The operator has undertaken to obtain an attestation from an independent person but none had been received when this claim was lodged with Scheldt Quay Customs Services NV.

Rebate: the operator states that no rebate, refund or other form of compensation was received for the amount claimed.

The claim is lodged against the consignments released for free circulation in the Union in the first half of 2026 that were produced at the installations named above.

Lodged with Scheldt Quay Customs Services NV on 31 July 2026.

## Basis of the charge

The charge is assessed on the emissions reported to the administering authority for the period and is payable whether or not the output is exported.

The amount above is the sum of the instalments settled with the administering authority during the period and does not include any penalty or interest.

## Records retained with this claim

- the assessment or allocation notice issued by the administering authority for Turkiye
- the bank advice evidencing the transfer of 3,260,000 TRY
- the operator's own reconciliation of the amount to the installations named

The claim relates to the charge borne in respect of Turkiye emissions trading system pilot phase and is lodged for the installations named at the head of this form.
