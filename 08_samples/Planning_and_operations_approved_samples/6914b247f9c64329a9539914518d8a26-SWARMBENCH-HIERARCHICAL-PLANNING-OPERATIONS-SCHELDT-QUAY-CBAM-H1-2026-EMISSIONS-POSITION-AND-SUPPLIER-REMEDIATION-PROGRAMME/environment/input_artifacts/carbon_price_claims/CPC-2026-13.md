Scheme: India coal cess on domestically raised coal

Period the payment relates to: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

## Basis of the charge

The scheme allocates a compliance obligation on the basis of verified emissions at installation level and settlement is annual with quarterly instalments.

The amount above is the sum of the instalments settled with the administering authority during the period and does not include any penalty or interest.
