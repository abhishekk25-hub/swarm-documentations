## Basis of the charge

The scheme allocates a compliance obligation on the basis of verified emissions at installation level and settlement is annual with quarterly instalments.

The amount is stated gross of any allowance surrendered free of charge and relates only to the units the operator purchased or the charge the operator paid in cash.
