# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-018
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-118, Gebze Ekstruzyon Sanayi A.S., Turkiye
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-118
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-118 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 11 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The team reviewed the monitoring plan, tested the activity data against source records, recalculated the emissions and examined the allocation between the goods the installation produces.

A virtual site visit was carried out under the conditions the accreditation body permits, supplemented by a physical inspection of the metering installation.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-118, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
- For installation INST-118, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-118, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Gebze Ekstruzyon Sanayi A.S. and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
