# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-034
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-135, Jorf Acides SA, Morocco
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-135
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-135 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 5 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The team reviewed the monitoring plan, tested the activity data against source records, recalculated the emissions and examined the allocation between the goods the installation produces.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-135, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-135, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-135, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Jorf Acides SA and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
