# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-007
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-107, Marmara Sicak Haddeleme A.S., Turkiye
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-107
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-107 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 13 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

A site visit was carried out. The team walked the process, inspected the metering points and the laboratory, and interviewed the shift supervisors and the environmental manager.

The risk analysis identified the manual transfer of weighbridge data as the principal inherent risk, and the testing was weighted accordingly.

## Findings

- For installation INST-107, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-107, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-107, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Marmara Sicak Haddeleme A.S. and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
