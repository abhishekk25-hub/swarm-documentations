# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-031
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-132, Arzew Azote SpA, Algeria
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-132
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-132 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 19 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

The engagement included a site visit of two days. Meter calibration certificates were inspected and the readings were traced from the field to the reported totals.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-132, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.
- For installation INST-132, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-132, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Arzew Azote SpA and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
