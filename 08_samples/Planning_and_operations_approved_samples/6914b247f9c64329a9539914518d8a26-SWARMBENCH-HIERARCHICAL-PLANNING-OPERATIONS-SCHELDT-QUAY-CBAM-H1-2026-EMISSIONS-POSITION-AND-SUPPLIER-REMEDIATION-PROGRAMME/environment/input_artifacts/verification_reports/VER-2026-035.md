# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-035
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-136, Damietta Ammonia Company SAE, Egypt
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-136
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-136 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: one outstanding. The attributed direct emissions stated for installation INST-136 could not be reconciled to the metered source data and the misstatement was not resolved before this report was issued.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 26 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

The engagement included a site visit of two days. Meter calibration certificates were inspected and the readings were traced from the field to the reported totals.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-136, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-136, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.
- For installation INST-136, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Damietta Ammonia Company SAE and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
