# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-030
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-131, Damietta Ammonia Company SAE, Egypt
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-131
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-131 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 13 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The scope of the engagement was the operator's communication in full, including the attribution of emissions to production processes and the derivation of the figures per tonne.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-131, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.
- For installation INST-131, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
- For installation INST-131, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Damietta Ammonia Company SAE and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
