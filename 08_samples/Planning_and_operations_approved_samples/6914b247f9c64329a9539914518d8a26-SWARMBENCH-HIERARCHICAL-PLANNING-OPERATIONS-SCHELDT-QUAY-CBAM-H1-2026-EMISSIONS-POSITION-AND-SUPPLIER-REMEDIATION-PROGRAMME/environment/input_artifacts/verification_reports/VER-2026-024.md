# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-024
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-124, Angul Primary Metal Ltd, India
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-124
Period covered by the verification: 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-124 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 2 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

The engagement included a site visit of two days. Meter calibration certificates were inspected and the readings were traced from the field to the reported totals.

The strategic analysis found the control environment adequate and the residual risk moderate, concentrated in the laboratory analysis of the process inputs.

## Findings

- For installation INST-124, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-124, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.
- For installation INST-124, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Angul Primary Metal Ltd and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
