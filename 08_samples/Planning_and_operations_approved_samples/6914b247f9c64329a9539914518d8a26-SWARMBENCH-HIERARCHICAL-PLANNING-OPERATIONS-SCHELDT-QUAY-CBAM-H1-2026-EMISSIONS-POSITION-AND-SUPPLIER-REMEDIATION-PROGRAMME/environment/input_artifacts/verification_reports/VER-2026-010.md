# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-010
Verification body: Nordkap Verifikasjon AS (VB-D)
Accreditation reference: ACC-NO-0091 issued by Norsk akkreditering
Installation verified: INST-110, Cua Lo Billet Company Ltd, Viet Nam
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-110
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-110 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Trondheim on 22 May 2026 by S. Aalvik for Nordkap Verifikasjon AS.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

A site visit was carried out. The team walked the process, inspected the metering points and the laboratory, and interviewed the shift supervisors and the environmental manager.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-110, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-110, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-110, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.

## Statement of independence

Nordkap Verifikasjon AS confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Cua Lo Billet Company Ltd and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-NO-0091.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
