# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-014
Verification body: Nordkap Verifikasjon AS (VB-D)
Accreditation reference: ACC-NO-0091 issued by Norsk akkreditering
Installation verified: INST-114, Vale do Paraopeba Ferro Gusa Ltda, Brazil
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-114
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-114 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Trondheim on 10 July 2026 by S. Aalvik for Nordkap Verifikasjon AS.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

The engagement included a site visit of two days. Meter calibration certificates were inspected and the readings were traced from the field to the reported totals.

The risk analysis identified the manual transfer of weighbridge data as the principal inherent risk, and the testing was weighted accordingly.

## Findings

- For installation INST-114, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-114, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-114, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.

## Statement of independence

Nordkap Verifikasjon AS confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Vale do Paraopeba Ferro Gusa Ltda and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-NO-0091.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
