# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-032
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-133, Arzew Azote SpA, Algeria
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-133
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-133 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 19 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

A site visit was performed and a sample of months was selected for detailed testing. For each month in the sample the reported quantity was agreed to the underlying weighbridge and meter records.

The risk analysis identified the manual transfer of weighbridge data as the principal inherent risk, and the testing was weighted accordingly.

## Findings

- For installation INST-133, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
- For installation INST-133, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-133, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Arzew Azote SpA and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
