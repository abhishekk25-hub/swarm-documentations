# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-005
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-105, Kalinga Sponge Works Pvt Ltd, India
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-105
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-105 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 24 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

A virtual site visit was carried out under the conditions the accreditation body permits, supplemented by a physical inspection of the metering installation.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-105, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
- For installation INST-105, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-105, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Kalinga Sponge Works Pvt Ltd and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
