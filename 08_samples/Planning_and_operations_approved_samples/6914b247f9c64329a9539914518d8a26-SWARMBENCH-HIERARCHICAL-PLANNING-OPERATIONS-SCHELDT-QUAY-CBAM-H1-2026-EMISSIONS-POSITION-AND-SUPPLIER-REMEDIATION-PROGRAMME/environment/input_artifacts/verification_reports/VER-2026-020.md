# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-020
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-120, Hanjiang Rolled Aluminium Co Ltd, China
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-120
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with qualifications.

The data submitted for installation INST-120 is accepted subject to the qualification recorded below, which the verification team was not able to close.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 27 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-120, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.
- For installation INST-120, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-120, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Hanjiang Rolled Aluminium Co Ltd and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
