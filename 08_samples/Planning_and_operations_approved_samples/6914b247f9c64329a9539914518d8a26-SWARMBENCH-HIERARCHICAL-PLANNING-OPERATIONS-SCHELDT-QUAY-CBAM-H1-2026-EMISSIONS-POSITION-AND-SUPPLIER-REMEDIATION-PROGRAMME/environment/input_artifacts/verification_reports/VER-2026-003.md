# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-003
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-103, Podunavlje Valjaonica d.o.o., Serbia
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-103
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-103 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 14 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The team reviewed the monitoring plan, tested the activity data against source records, recalculated the emissions and examined the allocation between the goods the installation produces.

A site visit was carried out. The team walked the process, inspected the metering points and the laboratory, and interviewed the shift supervisors and the environmental manager.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-103, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-103, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-103, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Podunavlje Valjaonica d.o.o. and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
