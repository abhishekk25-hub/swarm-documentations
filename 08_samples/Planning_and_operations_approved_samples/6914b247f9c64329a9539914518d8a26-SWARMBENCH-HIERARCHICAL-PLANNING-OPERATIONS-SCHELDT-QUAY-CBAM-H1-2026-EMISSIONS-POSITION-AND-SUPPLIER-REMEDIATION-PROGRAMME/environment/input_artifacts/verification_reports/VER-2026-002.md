# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-002
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-102, Dniprovske Chavun LLC, Ukraine
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-102
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-102 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 16 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The team reviewed the monitoring plan, tested the activity data against source records, recalculated the emissions and examined the allocation between the goods the installation produces.

A virtual site visit was carried out under the conditions the accreditation body permits, supplemented by a physical inspection of the metering installation.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-102, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.
- For installation INST-102, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-102, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Dniprovske Chavun LLC and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
