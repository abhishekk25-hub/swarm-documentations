# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-037
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-128, Ciments de Doukkala SA, Morocco
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-128
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: unable to verify.

The verification team was unable to verify the data submitted for installation INST-128 because the source records for the reporting period were not made available.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: three, none closed.

Note: this report was reissued. See VER-2026-027, which replaces it in full.

Signed at Duisburg on 24 June 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The team reviewed the monitoring plan, tested the activity data against source records, recalculated the emissions and examined the allocation between the goods the installation produces.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The risk analysis identified the manual transfer of weighbridge data as the principal inherent risk, and the testing was weighted accordingly.

## Findings

- For installation INST-128, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
- For installation INST-128, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-128, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Ciments de Doukkala SA and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
