## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

A site visit was performed and a sample of months was selected for detailed testing. For each month in the sample the reported quantity was agreed to the underlying weighbridge and meter records.

The risk analysis identified the manual transfer of weighbridge data as the principal inherent risk, and the testing was weighted accordingly.

## Findings

- For installation INST-105, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-105, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.
- For installation INST-105, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
