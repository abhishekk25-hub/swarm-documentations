# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-016
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-116, Matola Primary Aluminium SA, Mozambique
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-116
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-116 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 8 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The engagement covered the completeness of the source streams, the accuracy of the activity data, the correctness of the factors applied and the arithmetic of the attribution to goods.

A virtual site visit was carried out under the conditions the accreditation body permits, supplemented by a physical inspection of the metering installation.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-116, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-116, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.
- For installation INST-116, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Matola Primary Aluminium SA and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
