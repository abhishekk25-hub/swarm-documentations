# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-025
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-125, Bolu Klinker Fabrikasi A.S., Turkiye
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-125
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-125 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Antwerp on 3 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

The engagement included a site visit of two days. Meter calibration certificates were inspected and the readings were traced from the field to the reported totals.

The strategic analysis found the control environment adequate and the residual risk moderate, concentrated in the laboratory analysis of the process inputs.

## Findings

- For installation INST-125, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.
- For installation INST-125, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-125, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Bolu Klinker Fabrikasi A.S. and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
