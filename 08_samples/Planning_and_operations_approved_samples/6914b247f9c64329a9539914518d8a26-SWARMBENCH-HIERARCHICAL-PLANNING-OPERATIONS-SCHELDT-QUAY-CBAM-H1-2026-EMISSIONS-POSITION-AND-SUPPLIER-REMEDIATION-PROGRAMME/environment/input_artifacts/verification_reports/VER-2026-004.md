# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-004
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-104, Kalinga Sponge Works Pvt Ltd, India
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-104
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-104 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 9 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The scope of the engagement was the operator's communication in full, including the attribution of emissions to production processes and the derivation of the figures per tonne.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The strategic analysis found the control environment adequate and the residual risk moderate, concentrated in the laboratory analysis of the process inputs.

## Findings

- For installation INST-104, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.
- For installation INST-104, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-104, the monitoring plan version held on site was one revision behind the version lodged with the competent authority.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Kalinga Sponge Works Pvt Ltd and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
