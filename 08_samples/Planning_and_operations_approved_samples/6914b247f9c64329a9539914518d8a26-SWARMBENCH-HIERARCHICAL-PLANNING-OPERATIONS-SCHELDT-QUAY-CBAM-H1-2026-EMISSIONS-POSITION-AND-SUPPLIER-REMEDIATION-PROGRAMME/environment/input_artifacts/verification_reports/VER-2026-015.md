# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-015
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-115, Bharuch Fabricated Steel Ltd, India
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-115
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-115 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 23 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The analysis identified the substitution rules for missing data as the area of highest risk. The team tested every substitution applied in the period.

## Findings

- For installation INST-115, the internal review of the emissions report was performed by a person who had prepared part of it.
- For installation INST-115, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-115, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Bharuch Fabricated Steel Ltd and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
