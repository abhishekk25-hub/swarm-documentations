# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-001
Verification body: Antwerpen Verificatie NV (VB-A)
Accreditation reference: ACC-BE-0182 issued by BELAC
Installation verified: INST-101, Dniprovske Chavun LLC, Ukraine
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-101
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: unable to verify.

The verification team was unable to verify the data submitted for installation INST-101 because the source records for the reporting period were not made available.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: three, none closed.

Signed at Antwerp on 16 July 2026 by H. Vermeulen for Antwerpen Verificatie NV.

## Scope and method

The verification covered the activity data, the emission factors, the calculation methods, the application of the monitoring plan and the treatment of precursors.

The team carried out a site visit and a data walkthrough, and reperformed the calculation of the reported emissions independently of the operator's spreadsheet.

The risk analysis identified the manual transfer of weighbridge data as the principal inherent risk, and the testing was weighted accordingly.

## Findings

- For installation INST-101, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-101, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-101, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.

## Statement of independence

Antwerpen Verificatie NV confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Dniprovske Chavun LLC and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-BE-0182.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
