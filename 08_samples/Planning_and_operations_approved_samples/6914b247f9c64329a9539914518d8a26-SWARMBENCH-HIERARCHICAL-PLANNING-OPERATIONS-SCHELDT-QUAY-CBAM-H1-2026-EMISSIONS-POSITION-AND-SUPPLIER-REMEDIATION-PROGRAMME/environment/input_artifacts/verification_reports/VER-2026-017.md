# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-017
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-117, Sitra Smelter Company BSC, Bahrain
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-117
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-117 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 17 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The scope of the engagement was the operator's communication in full, including the attribution of emissions to production processes and the derivation of the figures per tonne.

A site visit was performed and a sample of months was selected for detailed testing. For each month in the sample the reported quantity was agreed to the underlying weighbridge and meter records.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-117, the allocation key between the two goods produced on the site was not documented in the plan, although it was applied consistently.
- For installation INST-117, the laboratory's uncertainty statement for one analysis did not carry the required signature.
- For installation INST-117, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Sitra Smelter Company BSC and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
