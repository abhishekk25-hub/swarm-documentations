# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-011
Verification body: Meridiana Verifica S.r.l. (VB-C)
Accreditation reference: ACC-IT-0233 issued by ACCREDIA
Installation verified: INST-111, Aktobe Chrome Alloys JSC, Kazakhstan
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-111
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-111 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Brescia on 7 July 2026 by L. Ferraro for Meridiana Verifica S.r.l..

## Scope and method

The team reviewed the monitoring plan, tested the activity data against source records, recalculated the emissions and examined the allocation between the goods the installation produces.

A site visit was carried out. The team walked the process, inspected the metering points and the laboratory, and interviewed the shift supervisors and the environmental manager.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-111, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-111, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.
- For installation INST-111, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.

## Statement of independence

Meridiana Verifica S.r.l. confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Aktobe Chrome Alloys JSC and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-IT-0233.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
