# Verification report

Report reference: VER-2026-033
Verification body: Rhenus Certification GmbH (VB-B)
Accreditation reference: ACC-DE-0417 issued by DAkkS
Installation verified: INST-134, Jorf Acides SA, Morocco
Operator communication verified: OER-2026-134
Period covered by the verification: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30

Opinion: verified with reasonable assurance.

The data submitted by the operator for installation INST-134 is free from material misstatement and the monitoring methodology has been applied as documented.

Material misstatements: none outstanding at the date of this report.

Non-conformities: two, both closed before issue.

Signed at Duisburg on 5 July 2026 by K. Brauer for Rhenus Certification GmbH.

## Scope and method

The scope of the engagement was the operator's communication in full, including the attribution of emissions to production processes and the derivation of the figures per tonne.

A virtual site visit was carried out under the conditions the accreditation body permits, supplemented by a physical inspection of the metering installation.

The principal risk identified was the allocation of emissions between the goods produced on the same site, which the team tested on a sample of months.

## Findings

- For installation INST-134, two weighbridge calibration certificates were not filed in the quality system, though the calibrations had been performed.
- For installation INST-134, the substitution log did not record the reason for one of the substitutions applied.
- For installation INST-134, one meter reading in the period was transcribed with a transposition that the operator corrected during the engagement.

## Statement of independence

Rhenus Certification GmbH confirms that it and the members of the verification team are independent of Jorf Acides SA and of any undertaking connected with it, that no member of the team has provided consultancy to the operator on the subject matter of this engagement, and that the engagement was carried out under the accreditation ACC-DE-0417.

The engagement was performed for the reporting period 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 and the conclusion above relates to that period only.
