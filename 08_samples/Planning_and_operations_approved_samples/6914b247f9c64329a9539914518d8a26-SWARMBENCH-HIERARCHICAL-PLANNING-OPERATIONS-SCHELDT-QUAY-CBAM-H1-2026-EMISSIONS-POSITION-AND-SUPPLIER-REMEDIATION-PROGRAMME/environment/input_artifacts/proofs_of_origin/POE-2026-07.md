# Supplier Declaration Of Origin

Reference: POE-2026-07
Presented against: declaration E-2026-055, item 1
Consignee: Lusatia Draht und Profil GmbH (IMP-07)
Goods: CN 7601, Unwrought aluminium
Net mass of the consignment: 268,400 kg

The goods described above are certified to originate in Bahrain.

Defect noted by the desk on receipt: the declaration was issued by a freight forwarder, which is not competent to certify origin.

The document is retained on the file for item 1 of declaration E-2026-055 and is not treated as settling the origin of those goods.

The issuing body has satisfied itself of the origin of the goods on the strength of the manufacturer's declaration and the works records produced to it.

Consignor of record: the seller named on the invoice for item 1 of declaration E-2026-055. Place of loading United Arab Emirates. Means of transport road trailer.
