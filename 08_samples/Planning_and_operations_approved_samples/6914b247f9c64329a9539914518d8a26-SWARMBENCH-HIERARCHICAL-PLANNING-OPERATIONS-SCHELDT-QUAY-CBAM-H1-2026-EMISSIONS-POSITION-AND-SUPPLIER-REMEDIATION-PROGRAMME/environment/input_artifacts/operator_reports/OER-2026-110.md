# Emissions statement for installation INST-110

Cua Lo Billet Company Ltd operates the installation identified as INST-110 at Nghe An in Viet Nam and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-110. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 7207 11 14 and the installation operates the route described as electric arc furnace, scrap based.

Over the period the installation produced 372 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 766 320 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. No indirect figure is stated here.

The processes inside the boundary are melting in the electric arc furnace, continuous casting.

Precursor consumption.
  No precursor input arises on this route.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 2.06 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

Activity data comes from the enterprise resource planning system and is reconciled at month end against the shift logs. The reconciliation for the period closed with no difference above the materiality the monitoring plan sets.

One analyser was out of service for eleven days in the period. The gap was filled with the conservative value the plan prescribes, which raises rather than lowers the reported figure.
