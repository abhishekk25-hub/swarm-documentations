# Emissions statement for installation INST-130

Damietta Ammonia Company SAE operates the installation identified as INST-130 at Damietta in Egypt and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-130. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 2814 10 00 and the installation operates the route described as steam reforming and ammonia synthesis.

Over the period the installation produced 720 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 1 735 200 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. Attributed indirect emissions were 136 800 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent.

The processes inside the boundary are steam reforming of natural gas, ammonia synthesis loop.

Precursor consumption.
  No precursor input arises on this route.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 2.41 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

The determination rests on the mass balance of the process, with the carbon entering in the raw materials and the fuels set against the carbon leaving in the product, in the residues and in the dust returned to the process. Quantities come from the plant historian.

No substitution of data was necessary during the period and no gap in the activity data arose.
