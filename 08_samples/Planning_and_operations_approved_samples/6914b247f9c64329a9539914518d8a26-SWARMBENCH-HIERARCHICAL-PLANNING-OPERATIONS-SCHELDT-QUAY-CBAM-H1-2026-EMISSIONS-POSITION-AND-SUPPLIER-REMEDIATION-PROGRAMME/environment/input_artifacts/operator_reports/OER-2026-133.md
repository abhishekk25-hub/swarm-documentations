# Arzew Azote SpA
## Communication of emissions data, reference OER-2026-133

This sheet is provided to our customs representative in the Union for installation INST-133 at Arzew, Algeria.

**Period covered.** 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026.

**Goods.** CN 3102 10 19. The route operated at this installation is urea synthesis.

**Quantities.** The installation produced 780,000 tonnes of the good in the period. Direct emissions attributed to that production were 304,200 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Indirect emissions attributed to that production were 117,000 tonnes of CO2 equivalent.

**Boundary.** The following processes sit inside the boundary: urea synthesis; granulation or prilling.

**Precursors.**
- Anhydrous ammonia, CN 2814 10 00, produced at installation INST-132; specific consumption 0.575 tonnes per tonne of product.

**Operator figure.** We state specific embedded emissions of 0.39 tonnes CO2e per tonne.

Issued 18 July 2026.

## Monitoring

The determination rests on the mass balance of the process, with the carbon entering in the raw materials and the fuels set against the carbon leaving in the product, in the residues and in the dust returned to the process. Quantities come from the plant historian.

No substitution of data was necessary during the period and no gap in the activity data arose.
