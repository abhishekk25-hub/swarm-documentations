# Nile Delta Rod and Bar SAE
## Communication of emissions data, reference OER-2026-113

This sheet is provided to our customs representative in the Union for installation INST-113 at Sadat City, Egypt.

**Period covered.** 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2025.

**Goods.** CN 7213. The route operated at this installation is hot rolling of semi-finished steel.

**Quantities.** The installation produced 176,000 tonnes of the good in the period. Direct emissions attributed to that production were 58,960 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Indirect emissions are not reported on this sheet.

**Boundary.** The following processes sit inside the boundary: reheating; hot rolling; coiling and finishing.

**Precursors.**
- Semi-finished products, CN 7207 11 14, produced at installation INST-105; specific consumption 1.046 tonnes per tonne of product.

**Operator figure.** We state specific embedded emissions of 0.335 tonnes CO2e per tonne.

Issued 18 July 2026.

## Monitoring

The site uses standard factors for the purchased fuels and site-specific factors for the process inputs, the latter derived from the analyses of the past twelve months. Where a measurement was missing the plan's substitution rule was applied.

The metering point on one of the ancillary boilers was replaced in the period. The old and the new meters were read on the day of the change and the quantities are continuous.
