# Emissions statement for installation INST-120

Hanjiang Rolled Aluminium Co Ltd operates the installation identified as INST-120 at Xiangyang in China and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-120. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 7608 and the installation operates the route described as aluminium extrusion.

Over the period the installation produced 66 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 38 412 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. No indirect figure is stated here.

The processes inside the boundary are billet heating, extrusion, drawing.

Precursor consumption.
  Unwrought aluminium, CN 7601, produced at installation INST-116; specific consumption 1.052 tonnes per tonne of product.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 0.582 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

Activity data comes from the enterprise resource planning system and is reconciled at month end against the shift logs. The reconciliation for the period closed with no difference above the materiality the monitoring plan sets.

A change of supplier for one of the fuels part way through the period brought a change of calorific value. Both values were applied to the quantities they actually relate to.
