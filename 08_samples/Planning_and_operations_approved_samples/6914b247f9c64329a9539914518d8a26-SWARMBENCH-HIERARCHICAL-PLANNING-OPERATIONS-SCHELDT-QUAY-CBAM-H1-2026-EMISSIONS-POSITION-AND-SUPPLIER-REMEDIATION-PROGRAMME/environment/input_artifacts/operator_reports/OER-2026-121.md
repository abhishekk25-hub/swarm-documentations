## Monitoring

The installation applies a measurement-based approach on the main stack and a calculation approach on the ancillary sources. The continuous emission measurement system is calibrated annually against a reference method and the availability over the period was above the threshold the plan requires.

One analyser was out of service for eleven days in the period. The gap was filled with the conservative value the plan prescribes, which raises rather than lowers the reported figure.
