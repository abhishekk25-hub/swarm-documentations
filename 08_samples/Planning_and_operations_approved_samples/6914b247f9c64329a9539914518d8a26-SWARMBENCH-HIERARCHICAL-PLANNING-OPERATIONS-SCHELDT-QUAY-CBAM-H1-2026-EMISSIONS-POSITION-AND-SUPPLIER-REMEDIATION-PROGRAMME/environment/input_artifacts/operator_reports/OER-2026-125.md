# Emissions statement for installation INST-125

Bolu Klinker Fabrikasi A.S. operates the installation identified as INST-125 at Bolu in Turkiye and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-125. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 2523 10 00 90 and the installation operates the route described as clinker burning.

Over the period the installation produced 1 240 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 1 031 680 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. Attributed indirect emissions were 59 520 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent.

The processes inside the boundary are raw meal preparation, clinker burning in the kiln, clinker cooling.

Precursor consumption.
  No precursor input arises on this route.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 0.832 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

Emissions are determined by the calculation-based approach set out in the monitoring plan approved for this installation. Fuel and material quantities are taken from the weighbridge and from the custody transfer meters, and calorific values and carbon contents are taken from the laboratory analyses carried out on the schedule the plan sets.

A change of supplier for one of the fuels part way through the period brought a change of calorific value. Both values were applied to the quantities they actually relate to.
