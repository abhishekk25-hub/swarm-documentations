# Ciments de Doukkala SA
## Communication of emissions data, reference OER-2026-128

This sheet is provided to our customs representative in the Union for installation INST-128 at El Jadida, Morocco.

**Period covered.** 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026.

**Goods.** CN 2523 29 00. The route operated at this installation is cement grinding.

**Quantities.** The installation produced 1,150,000 tonnes of the good in the period. Direct emissions attributed to that production were 43,700 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Indirect emissions attributed to that production were 67,850 tonnes of CO2 equivalent.

**Boundary.** The following processes sit inside the boundary: clinker grinding; blending and dispatch.

**Precursors.**
- Other clinker including grey clinker, CN 2523 10 00 90, produced at installation INST-127; specific consumption 0.735 tonnes per tonne of product.

**Operator figure.** We state specific embedded emissions of 0.038 tonnes CO2e per tonne.

Issued 18 July 2026.

## Monitoring

Activity data comes from the enterprise resource planning system and is reconciled at month end against the shift logs. The reconciliation for the period closed with no difference above the materiality the monitoring plan sets.

One analyser was out of service for eleven days in the period. The gap was filled with the conservative value the plan prescribes, which raises rather than lowers the reported figure.
