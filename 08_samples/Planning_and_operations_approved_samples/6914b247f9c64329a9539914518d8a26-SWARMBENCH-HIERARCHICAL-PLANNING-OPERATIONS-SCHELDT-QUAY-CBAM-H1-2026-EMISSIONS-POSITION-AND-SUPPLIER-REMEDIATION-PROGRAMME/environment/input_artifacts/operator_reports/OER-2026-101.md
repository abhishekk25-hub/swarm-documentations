## Monitoring

Emissions are determined by the calculation-based approach set out in the monitoring plan approved for this installation. Fuel and material quantities are taken from the weighbridge and from the custody transfer meters, and calorific values and carbon contents are taken from the laboratory analyses carried out on the schedule the plan sets.

A change of supplier for one of the fuels part way through the period brought a change of calorific value. Both values were applied to the quantities they actually relate to.
