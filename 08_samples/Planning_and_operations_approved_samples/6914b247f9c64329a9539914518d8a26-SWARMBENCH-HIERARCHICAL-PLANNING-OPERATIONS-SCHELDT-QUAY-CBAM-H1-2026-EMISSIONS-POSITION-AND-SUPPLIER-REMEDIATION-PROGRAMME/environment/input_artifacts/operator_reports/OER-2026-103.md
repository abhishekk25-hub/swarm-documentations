# Podunavlje Valjaonica d.o.o.
## Communication of emissions data, reference OER-2026-103

This sheet is provided to our customs representative in the Union for installation INST-103 at Smederevo, Serbia.

**Period covered.** 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026.

**Goods.** CN 7208. The route operated at this installation is hot rolling of semi-finished steel.

**Quantities.** The installation produced 386,000 tonnes of the good in the period. Direct emissions attributed to that production were 140,504 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Indirect emissions are not reported on this sheet.

**Boundary.** The following processes sit inside the boundary: reheating; hot rolling; coiling and finishing.

**Precursors.**
- Semi-finished products, CN 7207 11 14, produced at installation INST-102; specific consumption 1.062 tonnes per tonne of product.

**Operator figure.** We state specific embedded emissions of 0.364 tonnes CO2e per tonne.

Issued 18 July 2026.

## Monitoring

Quantities of fuel are taken from supplier invoices reconciled monthly against tank dips. Process emissions are calculated from the carbonate content of the raw meal, sampled daily and composited weekly for analysis.

No substitution of data was necessary during the period and no gap in the activity data arose.
