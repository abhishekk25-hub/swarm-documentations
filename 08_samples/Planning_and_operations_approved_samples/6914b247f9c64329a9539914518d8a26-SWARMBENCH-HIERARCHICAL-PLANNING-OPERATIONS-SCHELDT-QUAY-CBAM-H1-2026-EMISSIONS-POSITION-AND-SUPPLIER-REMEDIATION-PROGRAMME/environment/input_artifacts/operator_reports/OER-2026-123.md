# Angul Primary Metal Ltd
## Communication of emissions data, reference OER-2026-123

This sheet is provided to our customs representative in the Union for installation INST-123 at Angul, India.

**Period covered.** 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026.

**Goods.** CN 7601. The route operated at this installation is primary aluminium electrolysis.

**Quantities.** The installation produced 460,000 tonnes of the good in the period. Direct emissions attributed to that production were 1,444,400 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Indirect emissions are not reported on this sheet.

**Boundary.** The following processes sit inside the boundary: anode production; electrolysis; casting.

**Precursors.**
- None consumed.

**Operator figure.** We state specific embedded emissions of 3.14 tonnes CO2e per tonne.

Issued 18 July 2026.

## Monitoring

Emissions are determined by the calculation-based approach set out in the monitoring plan approved for this installation. Fuel and material quantities are taken from the weighbridge and from the custody transfer meters, and calorific values and carbon contents are taken from the laboratory analyses carried out on the schedule the plan sets.

One analyser was out of service for eleven days in the period. The gap was filled with the conservative value the plan prescribes, which raises rather than lowers the reported figure.
