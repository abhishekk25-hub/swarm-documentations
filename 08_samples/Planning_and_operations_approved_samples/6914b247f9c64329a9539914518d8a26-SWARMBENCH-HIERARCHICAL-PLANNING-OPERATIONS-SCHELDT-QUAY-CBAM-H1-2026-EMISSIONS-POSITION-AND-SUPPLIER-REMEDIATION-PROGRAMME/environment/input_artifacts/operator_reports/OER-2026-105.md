# Emissions statement for installation INST-105

Kalinga Sponge Works Pvt Ltd operates the installation identified as INST-105 at Rourkela in India and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-105. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 7207 11 14 and the installation operates the route described as electric arc furnace with direct reduced iron.

Over the period the installation produced 398 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 204 970 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. No indirect figure is stated here.

The processes inside the boundary are melting in the electric arc furnace, continuous casting.

Precursor consumption.
  Ferrous products obtained by direct reduction of iron ore an, CN 7203, produced at installation INST-104; specific consumption 1.120 tonnes per tonne of product.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 0.515 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

The determination rests on the mass balance of the process, with the carbon entering in the raw materials and the fuels set against the carbon leaving in the product, in the residues and in the dust returned to the process. Quantities come from the plant historian.

The laboratory changed the method for the carbon content of one input stream in March. The results of both methods were compared over a month of parallel running and the difference was below the plan's threshold.
