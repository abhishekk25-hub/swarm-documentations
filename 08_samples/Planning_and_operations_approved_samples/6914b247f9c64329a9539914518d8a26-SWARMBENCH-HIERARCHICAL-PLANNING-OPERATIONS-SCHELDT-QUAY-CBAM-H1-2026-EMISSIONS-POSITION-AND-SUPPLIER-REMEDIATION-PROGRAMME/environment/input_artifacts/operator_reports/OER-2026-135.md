# Emissions statement for installation INST-135

Jorf Acides SA operates the installation identified as INST-135 at Jorf Lasfar in Morocco and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-135. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 3105 20 10 and the installation operates the route described as compound fertiliser granulation.

Over the period the installation produced 560 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 145 600 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. Attributed indirect emissions were 67 200 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent.

The processes inside the boundary are neutralisation, granulation, blending.

Precursor consumption.
  Anhydrous ammonia, CN 2814 10 00, produced at installation INST-130; consumption expressed as 15.0 per cent of the mass of product.
  Nitric acid; sulphonitric acids, CN 2808 00 00, produced at installation INST-134; consumption expressed as 32.0 per cent of the mass of product.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 0.26 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

Emissions are determined by the calculation-based approach set out in the monitoring plan approved for this installation. Fuel and material quantities are taken from the weighbridge and from the custody transfer meters, and calorific values and carbon contents are taken from the laboratory analyses carried out on the schedule the plan sets.

A change of supplier for one of the fuels part way through the period brought a change of calorific value. Both values were applied to the quantities they actually relate to.
