# Gebze Ekstruzyon Sanayi A.S.
## Communication of emissions data, reference OER-2026-118

This sheet is provided to our customs representative in the Union for installation INST-118 at Gebze, Turkiye.

**Period covered.** 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026.

**Goods.** CN 7604 21 00. The route operated at this installation is aluminium extrusion.

**Quantities.** The installation produced 84,000 tonnes of the good in the period. Direct emissions attributed to that production were 43,176 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. Indirect emissions are not reported on this sheet.

**Boundary.** The following processes sit inside the boundary: billet heating; extrusion; ageing.

**Precursors.**
- Unwrought aluminium, CN 7601, produced at installation INST-117; specific consumption 1.045 tonnes per tonne of product.

**Operator figure.** We state specific embedded emissions of 0.514 tonnes CO2e per tonne.

Issued 18 July 2026.

## Monitoring

The determination rests on the mass balance of the process, with the carbon entering in the raw materials and the fuels set against the carbon leaving in the product, in the residues and in the dust returned to the process. Quantities come from the plant historian.

One analyser was out of service for eleven days in the period. The gap was filled with the conservative value the plan prescribes, which raises rather than lowers the reported figure.
