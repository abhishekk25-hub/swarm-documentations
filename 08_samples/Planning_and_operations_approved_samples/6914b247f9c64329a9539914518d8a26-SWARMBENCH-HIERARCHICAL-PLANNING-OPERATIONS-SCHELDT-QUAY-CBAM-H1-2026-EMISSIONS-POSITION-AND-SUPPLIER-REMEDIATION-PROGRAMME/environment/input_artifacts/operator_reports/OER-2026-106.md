## Monitoring

The determination rests on the mass balance of the process, with the carbon entering in the raw materials and the fuels set against the carbon leaving in the product, in the residues and in the dust returned to the process. Quantities come from the plant historian.

Two shifts in the period were logged manually after a failure of the historian and the quantities for those shifts were substituted using the average of the surrounding week, as the monitoring plan allows.
