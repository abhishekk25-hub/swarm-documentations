# Emissions statement for installation INST-115

Bharuch Fabricated Steel Ltd operates the installation identified as INST-115 at Bharuch in India and provides the figures below for the goods it has supplied to the Union.

Statement reference OER-2026-115. The monitoring period runs from 1 January 2026 to 30 June 2026. The good is CN 7326 90 98 and the installation operates the route described as fabrication of steel articles.

Over the period the installation produced 96 000 tonnes of the good and attributed 40 896 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent to that production as direct emissions. No indirect figure is stated here.

The processes inside the boundary are cutting and forming, welding, surface treatment.

Precursor consumption.
  Flat-rolled products of iron or non-alloy steel, CN 7208, produced at installation INST-107; consumption expressed as 103.0 per cent of the mass of product.

The operator's own figure for specific embedded emissions is 0.426 tonnes CO2e per tonne of good.

Dated 15/07/2026.

## Monitoring

The determination rests on the mass balance of the process, with the carbon entering in the raw materials and the fuels set against the carbon leaving in the product, in the residues and in the dust returned to the process. Quantities come from the plant historian.

One analyser was out of service for eleven days in the period. The gap was filled with the conservative value the plan prescribes, which raises rather than lowers the reported figure.
