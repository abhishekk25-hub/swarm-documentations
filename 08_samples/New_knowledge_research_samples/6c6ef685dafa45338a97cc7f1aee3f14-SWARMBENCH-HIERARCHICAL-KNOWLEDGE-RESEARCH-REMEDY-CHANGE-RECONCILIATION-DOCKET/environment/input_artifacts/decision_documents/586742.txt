                                UNITED STATES ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY
                                                    REGION 2

  DATE:    February 6, 2020
SUBJECT:   Record of Decision
           Matlack Inc. Site, Woolwich Township, Gloucester County, New Jersey
  FROM:    Stephanie Vaughn, Chief, Mega Projects Section
           Special Projects Branch, Superfund and Emergency Management Division

     TO:   File

           A Record of Decision for the above-referenced site was signed on September 29, 2017. It has
           since been determined that an inadvertent, administrative error was made in the document.
           Specifically, errors were found in Appendix 2, Table 7 of the document, which lists the
           remediation goals for the site.

           This error has not had any effect on the design of the remedy, and no action beyond replacing
           the incorrect table with the correct one is needed to correct the document. These statements
           are supported by the following:

                  -   The proposed plan for the site, which was released on August 23, 2017, as well as the
                      Feasibility Study Report, which formed the basis of the proposed plan, included the
                      correct version of the table. As such, all comments received during the proposed plan
                      public comment period, were based on review of documents including the correct table.
                  -   The language in the body of the ROD itself says, “EPA has adopted the preliminary
                      remediation goals identified in the Proposed Plan as the final Remediation Goals (RGs)
                      for Site-related COCs.” As such, no changes to the text of the ROD are needed.
                  -   The inadvertant error was discovered during preparation of plans to conduct the pre-
                      design investigation to support the design of the remedy. As such, no design plans have
                      moved forward using incorrect information.
           Based on the above, the incorrect version of the table in the final version of the ROD has been
           replaced with the correct one (attached) and the official Administrative Record for the site has
           been appropriately updated.

           Please contact Stephanie Vaughn at 212-637-3914 if you have any questions or concerns.

           Attachment
