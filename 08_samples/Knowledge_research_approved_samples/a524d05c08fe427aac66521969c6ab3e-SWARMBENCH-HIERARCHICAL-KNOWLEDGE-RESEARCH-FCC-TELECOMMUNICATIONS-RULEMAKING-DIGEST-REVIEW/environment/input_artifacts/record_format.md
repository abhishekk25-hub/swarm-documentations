# Per-release digest layout

Every release's digest under `digests/aNN.md` follows the same shape so the shelf stays uniform. Write in
your own words; do not paste blocks of the record back.

## Heading

Start the file with a single heading line carrying the digest id, the rule subject, and the document
number, for example (illustrative placeholder, not one of the releases on the roster):

`# aNN - Example Rule Subject - 2025-00000`

## Sections

Work through these, each as a short headed paragraph (a sentence-fragment header is fine):

- **Subject and service** - what the rule is about, which spectrum bands, licensee classes, or equipment
  it reaches, and the radio service it falls under (wireless, broadcast, satellite, wireline, public
  safety, or media), as the record states them.
- **Action taken** - the kind of Commission action (Report and Order, Memorandum Opinion and Order, Order
  on Reconsideration, or Further Notice) and the substance of the rules it adopts.
- **Rules and obligations** - the specific limits, eligibility, technical requirements, deadlines, or
  obligations the release imposes, with the numbers the record gives (power limits, frequencies, dates,
  fees).
- **Problem and record** - the problem the Commission was addressing and the comments, petitions, or
  record it was responding to.
- **Reasoning** - the reasoning the Commission gave for the choices it made, from the record.
- **What sets it apart** - the bands, licensees, and figures that anchor this release, so the digest could
  not be mistaken for any other release on the shelf.

## Length and honesty

A serious digest usually runs a few hundred words. Do not invent anything the record does not state. The
digest must reflect the release in front of you and nothing else - no band, limit, or finding the record
does not carry, and nothing carried over from another release.
