We license a sixty-unit public-domain reading set across four school-year bands and three
subject groupings. I need evidence showing which ideas the set teaches more than once, at
more than one band, and what changes between one treatment and the next. Work from the staged
material in /input_artifacts; no network needed.

Produce four things.

First, a concept sheet for every unit at /logs/agent/units/<unit_id>.json. Summarise it, then
record exactly three concepts it treats. Anchor each to a verbatim quotation
from that unit's own passage file, say what the passage does with the concept, and rate how
demanding the treatment is on the five-level scale in the standard, with your reason. Rate
the passage in front of you, not the band it sits in.

Second, a dossier for each concept at /logs/agent/concepts/<concept_id>.json. Twenty-four of
them. Gather every unit that recorded the concept into rungs ordered by band, each saying what
that treatment adds over the one below. Compute the bands covered, the span
class and the regression flags from the rungs.

Third, /logs/agent/progression_index.json, counting what the delivered files carry.

Fourth, /logs/agent/articulation_report.md, in the five sections the standard names.

/input_artifacts/articulation_standard.md gives the field contract, the demand scale, the
derivation rules and the counts the finished set must satisfy. Follow it literally.

Two things matter most. A dossier's rungs must be exactly the observations that named it,
with the same bands and levels. And a concept must be one idea: this corpus uses the same
word for different things in different clusters.
