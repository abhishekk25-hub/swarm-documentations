# Per-trial digest layout

Every trial's digest under `digests/tNN.md` follows the same shape so the trial tracker reads
consistently. Write in your own words; do not paste blocks of the record back.

## Heading

Start the file with a single heading line carrying the report id, the sponsor or trial name, and
the registry identifier, for example:

`# <report id> - <trial acronym or lead sponsor, as the record gives it> - <registry identifier>`

## Sections

Work through these, each as a short headed paragraph (a sentence-fragment header is fine):

- **Trial and population** - the sponsor or trial name, the condition and phase, whether the
  design was randomized, blinded, and controlled, and against what comparator.
- **Question and endpoints** - the pre-specified primary endpoint, the key secondary endpoints,
  and the hypothesis they tested. Where the record is not a primary-efficacy read-out, say what
  analysis or question it addresses instead.
- **Population and exposure** - the number randomized, the arms and their dosing or intervention,
  the follow-up window, and the eligibility the record states.
- **Results and safety** - the primary-endpoint effect with its confidence interval or p-value,
  the secondary signals worth flagging, and the notable adverse events or discontinuations. Name
  the figures as the record states them.
- **Verdict** - whether the trial met, missed, came in mixed on, or was terminated before its
  pre-specified primary endpoint, and the basis the record gives.
- **Why it matters** - one or two lines on what makes this read-out notable.

## Length and honesty

A serious digest usually runs a few hundred words. Where the record genuinely does not settle a
point (for example, a primary effect size for a trial stopped early), say so in a line rather than
inventing a figure. The digest must reflect the record in front of you and nothing else.
