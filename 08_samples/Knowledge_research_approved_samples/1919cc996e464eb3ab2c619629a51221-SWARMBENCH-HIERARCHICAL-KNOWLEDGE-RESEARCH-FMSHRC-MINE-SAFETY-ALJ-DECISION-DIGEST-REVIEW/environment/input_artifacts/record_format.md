Use these six labeled sections in every decision digest. Write in your own words and support each section with particulars from that decision.

1. Matter identity and posture
   Identify the parties or mine, complete docket, proceeding type, and how the matter reached the administrative law judge.

2. Enforcement question
   State the citation, order, mandatory standard, Mine Act provision, protected activity, or other claim actually at issue.

3. Operative facts and proof
   Reconstruct the condition, inspection, discipline, or event. Include the measurements, exhibits, testimony, notice, knowledge, and credibility choices that mattered.

4. Governing test and analysis
   Explain the legal test the judge applied and how the proof affected violation, gravity, negligence, significant-and-substantial, unwarrantable-failure, discrimination, individual-liability, or penalty findings as applicable.

5. Findings and disposition
   Distinguish each citation, order, or claim sustained, modified, vacated, dismissed, or otherwise resolved and give the judge's reason.

6. Penalty or remedy
   State the assessed amount and penalty-factor reasoning, or the reinstatement, backpay, dismissal, settlement, or other relief actually ordered.
