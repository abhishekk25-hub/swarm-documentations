The enforcement conference needs a bench book that shows why each ALJ ruling came out as it did. For every
matter, trace the cited standard or protected activity through the proof the judge accepted and into the
violation, penalty, reinstatement, backpay, or dismissal. A docket or citation copied without analysis is not useful.

Work in `/workspace`. The shelf is `/environment/input_artifacts/decisions/f01.txt` through `f72.txt`.
`/environment/input_artifacts/case_roster.csv` controls the labels and `/environment/input_artifacts/source_manifest.csv` preserves provenance. Derive the technical,
evidentiary, and legal content from the ruling body.

Place the cards in `/logs/agent/output/digests/`, following `/environment/input_artifacts/record_format.md`. Start each with its `f` label,
parties or mine, and complete docket. Account for the mandatory standard or claimed protected activity; the
condition, inspection, discipline, or other operative event; measurements, exhibits, testimony, and stated
credibility grounds; the Mine Act test actually reached; negligence or motivation; S&S,
unwarrantable-failure, section 105(c), section 110(c), and penalty-factor treatment when applicable; the
precise disposition; and the assessed penalty or other relief. Paraphrase rather than copying. Keep each
ruling's facts inside its own card.

When the record turns on mine operations, preserve the physical mechanism instead of flattening it into a
generic violation. That may mean ventilation readings, methane or dust control, roof and rib conditions,
mobile-equipment travelways, guarding, lockout, escapeways, preshift or onshift examinations, blasting,
electrical installations, training records, or inspector observations. Explain how the measured condition,
miners exposed, duration, likelihood, and expected injury informed gravity and S&S analysis.
