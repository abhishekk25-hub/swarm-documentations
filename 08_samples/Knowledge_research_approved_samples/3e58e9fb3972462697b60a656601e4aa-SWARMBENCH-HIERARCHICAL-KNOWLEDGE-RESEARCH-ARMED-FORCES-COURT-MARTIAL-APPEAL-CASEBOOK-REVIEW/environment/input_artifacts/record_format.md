# Per-case record entry layout

Every case's record entry under `case_files/mNN.md` follows the same shape so the division's running
record stays uniform. Write in your own words; do not paste blocks of the case back.

## Heading

Start the file with a single heading line carrying the report id, the convicted member, and the case
number, for example:

`# m07 - United States v. Ramirez - No. 24-0132/AR`

## Sections

Work through these, each as a short headed paragraph (a sentence-fragment header is fine):

- **Member and forum** - who the convicted member is, the service, whether the trial was a general or
  special court-martial, and the convening posture the case describes.
- **Convictions and sentence** - the offenses and the punitive articles the member stands convicted
  under, and the confinement, discharge, forfeitures, or reduction adjudged, as the case states them.
- **Error decided** - the one error the defense raised that the court agreed to decide (for example a
  bad members instruction, insufficient proof of guilt, deficient defense counsel, command pressure on
  the process, an unlawful search or coerced statement, a denied speedy trial, a guilty plea taken
  without a proper inquiry, or a punishment too severe for the offense).
- **Measure and reasoning** - the yardstick the court used to measure that error and how it worked to a
  result, including the authority it relied on. Name it as the case states it.
- **Result on findings and punishment** - what the court did with the findings and the punishment:
  left them standing, wiped a conviction out, cut or re-figured the punishment, ordered the case
  retried, or sent it back down, and the basis it gives.
- **Punishment relief** - whether the court touched the punishment at all, and if so how, or left it
  standing.

## Length and honesty

A serious entry usually runs a few hundred words. Where the case genuinely does not settle a point,
say so in a line rather than inventing something. The entry must reflect the case in front of you and
nothing else.
