Brief summary
  The MORDOR trial found that biannual distribution of azithromycin to children 1-59 months old reduced child mortality.
  The World Health Organization (WHO) released conditional guidelines for this intervention, which include targeting azithromycin distributions to children 1-11 months of age in high mortality settings.Targeting treatment to children 1-11 months old could reduce antimicrobial resistance by limiting antibiotic distributions while treating children at the highest mortality risk.
  However, this targeted intervention has not yet been tested.
  
  The AVENIR mortality/resistance trial aims to assess the efficacy of age-based targeting of biannual azithromycin distribution on mortality as well as determine the impact of age-based targeting on antimicrobial resistance.

Detailed description
  In the mortality/resistance trial, 3,350 communities in the Dosso and Tahoua regions of Niger will be randomized to one of three arms: 1) azithro 1-11: biannual oral azithromycin to children 1-11 months old with biannual oral placebo to children 12-59 months old, 2) azithro 1-59: biannual oral azithromycin to children 1-59 months old, or 3) placebo: biannual oral placebo to children 1-59 months old.
  Interventions will be delivered biannually through a door-to-door census.
  Mortality will also be monitored through biannual census data collection, which will be used to adaptively allocation treatment assignments after the first year.
  Communities will retain an allocation for 4 distributions before being re-randomized.
  
  Antimicrobial resistance will be monitored using cluster sampling of treated and untreated children and adults in the Dosso region.

Arms and interventions
  Arm: Azithro 1-11 (ACTIVE_COMPARATOR)
    Biannual weight- or height-based dose of oral azithromycin suspension to children 1-11 months old and oral placebo to children 12-59 months old
  Arm: Azithro 1-59 (ACTIVE_COMPARATOR)
    Biannual weight- or height-based dose of oral azithromycin suspension to children 1-59 months old
  Arm: Placebo (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR)
    Biannual weight- or height-based dose of oral placebo to children 1-59 months old
  Intervention: DRUG Azithromycin
  Intervention: OTHER Placebo

Outcome measures
  [Primary outcome] All-cause mortality (1-59 months old)
    Description: Mortality rate (deaths per 1,000 person-years at risk) among children 1-59 months of age, comparing the azithro 1-59 and placebo arms.
    Time frame: 2.5 years from the first enrollment
  [Primary outcome] All-cause mortality (1-11 months old)
    Description: Mortality rate (deaths per 1,000 person-years at risk) among children 1-11 months of age, comparing the azithro 1-11 and placebo arms.
    Time frame: 2.5 years from the first enrollment
  [Primary outcome] All-cause mortality (12-59 months old)
    Description: Mortality rate (deaths per 1,000 person-years at risk) among children 12-59 months of age with rates compared between azithro 1-11 and azithro 1-59 communities.
    Time frame: 2.5 years from the first enrollment
  [Primary outcome] Prevalence of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides - nasopharyngeal swabs (1-59 months old)
    Description: Prevalence of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides including those determinants known to be found in Streptococcus pneumoniae, Streptococcus pyogenes, and Staphylococcus aureus from nasopharyngeal swabs in children 1-59 months old.
    Time frame: After 4 distributions (approximately 24 months)
  [Primary outcome] Load of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides - rectal swabs (1-59 months old)
    Description: Load of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides including those determinants known to be found in Campylobacter, Salmonella spp., Shigella spp., and Escherichia coli from rectal swabs in children 1-59 months old.
    Time frame: After 4 distributions (approximately 24 months)
  [Secondary outcome] All-cause mortality (12-59 months old)
    Description: Mortality rate (deaths per 1,000 person-years at risk) among children ages 12-59 months over 2.5 years, comparing the azithro 1-11 and placebo arms.
    Time frame: 2.5 years from first enrollment
  [Secondary outcome] All-cause mortality (1-11 months old )
    Description: Mortality rate (deaths per 1,000 person-years at risk) among children ages 1-11 months over 2.5 years, comparing the azithro 1-11 and azithro 1-59 arms.
    Time frame: 2.5 years from first enrollment
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Region
    Description: Mortality rate compared across study regions (Dosso, Tahoua, Maradi, Zinder, and Tillaberi).
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Number of previous community distributions
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups based on the number of previous community azithromycin distributions during the study period.
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Timing of distribution
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups based on timing of azithromycin distribution rounds during the study period.
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Seasonality
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups based on seasonality of azithromycin distribution rounds during the study period.
    Time frame: 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Other Intervention, MORDOR
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups based on previous participation in MORDOR trials.
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Other Intervention, Seasonal Malaria Chemoprevention
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups based on participation in and coverage of Seasonal Malaria Chemoprevention (SMC).
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Other Intervention, trachoma distributions
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups based on participation in trachoma distributions of azithromycin.
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Mortality rate by subgroup: Nutritional status
    Description: Mortality rate compared across subgroups base on nutritional status, as defined by the following three measurements: MUAC (mid-upper arm circumference), height interval, and weight will be combined to report on overall nutritional status.
    Time frame: Over 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Prevalence of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides from nasopharyngeal swabs and load of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides from rectal swabs
    Description: Prevalence of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides from nasopharyngeal swabs and load of genetic determinants of resistance to macrolides from rectal swabs in:
      
      
      - Children 7-12 years old at 24 months from baseline 
      - Caregivers/guardians of eligible children at 24 months from baseline
    Time frame: After 4 distributions (approximately 24 months)
  [Secondary outcome] Prevalence of genetic determinants of resistance to other antibiotic classes
    Description: Prevalence of genetic determinants of resistance to other antibiotic classes from nasopharyngeal swabs and rectal swabs in all samples.
    Time frame: After 4 distributions (approximately 24 months)
  [Secondary outcome] Proportion of pneumococcal isolates - children 1-59 months
    Description: Proportion of macrolide resistant pneumococcal isolates from nasopharyngeal swabs in children 1-59 months.
    Time frame: After 4 distributions (approximately 24 months)
  [Secondary outcome] Prevalence of malaria as assessed by RDT
    Description: Prevalence of malaria as assessed by RDT during sample collections at baseline and 24 months.
    Time frame: After 4 distributions (approximately 24 months)
  [Secondary outcome] Program costs
    Description: Program costs as captured by routine administrative data collection during the study period and by micro-costing activities.
    Time frame: 2.5 years
  [Secondary outcome] Cost-effectiveness
    Description: Cost-effectiveness measured by estimated program costs and mortality, compared across arms.
    Time frame: 2.5 years
