Meeting addressed client rejection feedback via standardized verification protocols, updated metadata schemas, and improved evaluation criteria transparency.


Verifier Configuration and Logical Standards
* Verifier type updated to 'hybrid' in TOML files (Ruturaj).
* Reward scores transitioned from reward.txt to reward.json to include metadata such as structural, reward hacking, and partial oracle check scores.
* Verifier composition mandated: content checks must comprise at least 40% and structural checks no more than 60% of test cases.
* Content checks must prioritize analytical verification rather than merely confirming input text presence in the output (S G Mathavan).
* Scoring logic should remain simple, utilizing standard addition or averaging, to avoid escalation due to overly complex custom gates.


Alignment and Consistency Protocols
* Single-agent and multi-agent runs must utilize identical versions of verifier.py to prevent validation gaps.
* Rubrics manifest (rubrics_manifest.json) must encompass all test cases defined in verifier.py.
* Verifier logic, including scoring mechanisms and weightings, must be human-readable and explainable in the manifest file.


Debugging and Evaluation Procedures
* Adding print statements to verifier.py required to log structural check results, scores, and LLM justifications for debugging.
* Hollow submissions advised as a testing mechanism to ensure the verifier accurately assesses requirements and correctly rejects incomplete tasks.
